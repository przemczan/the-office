Sections by state

Active first, then Shaping, each as today's cards, two across on a wide page. Done and Dropped sit at the foot, each one card with a fold, folded at first, a row per Initiative. No two states share a list, and a Dropped one is never beside an Active one.

Order and cards

Active and Shaping: newest first, as today. Done and Dropped: the one that ended last on top, by a new doneAt or droppedAt, filled in for those already ended from their Tasks' recorded moves where there are any; one with no date sits last. An Active card drops its badge (the section says it) and adds what moves now: Tasks waiting on you, in progress and blocked. A Shaping card shows its Tasks so far instead of progress. An ended row: name, Projects, final count and when it ended.

Folds, counts and empty states

A fold is remembered in this browser, per section, as the Work board's groups are. Each section counts the Initiatives it shows. Active and Shaping always show, saying what would fill them when empty; Done and Dropped leave the page when they have none. The note of other Projects stays above, as today.

About the mock

Its data follows Initiative and Task in packages/contracts, with the proposed doneAt and droppedAt. The Project filter, the folds and the State filter's chips work; opening an Initiative shows a toast. It uses the Geist fonts only where they are installed, and makes no network requests.
