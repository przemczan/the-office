A board, as the Tasks tab has

The same filter bar, columns and cards as the Tasks tab: Shaping, Active and Done side by side, left to right as an Initiative moves. A card is one link with an edge in its state's colour: amber while a Task waits on you, green while one is in progress or once Done, red once Dropped. No two states share a list: the Dropped ones are folded at the foot of Done, out of the way of the Active ones.

An Archive, so it stays short for years

A Done or Dropped Initiative leaves the board after the Archive period (30 days, the one Tasks use), or at once by Archive in its card's More menu. It goes to the Archived tab, beside the archived Tasks: searched, filtered by Done or Dropped, by month, a page at a time. Restore puts it back. The board then holds what is moving and what just ended, whether there are ten Initiatives or ten thousand.

Find one

Search reaches names and outcomes, and the Waiting on you chip keeps the Initiatives with a Task waiting on you; both are kept in the address, as the Tasks tab's filters are. A search that matches archived Initiatives says how many and opens the archive on the same words. The Project filter narrows everything, with the note of other Projects.

About the mock

Its data follows Initiative and Task in packages/contracts, with the proposed endedAt and archivedAt. “Two years in” adds about 1,240 made-up Initiatives. Search, the chip, the folds, Archive, Restore, the pages and the List's State filter work; opening an Initiative shows a toast. It uses the Geist fonts only where they are installed, and makes no network requests.
