The switch and the address

Group by Initiative is a switch at the end of the filter bar's first row, apart from the filters: it lays the board out and narrows nothing. It is kept in the address as ?group=initiative, beside the filters. Clear and the Saved views leave it as it is; a Saved view holds filters only.

A group

One per Initiative with a card on the board: its header, then its Tasks in the six columns. The header shows the name first, then the state, the Projects, the progress over every Task of the Initiative, the cards it has on the board (or how many show of them), and Open Initiative. No Initiative comes last.

Folding and order

A group folds to its header and its cards per column. The fold is remembered in this browser per group, never in the address; a Done or Dropped Initiative's group starts folded. Groups follow the Initiatives tab's order, newest first, Active ones before those that ended.

Filters and drag

The Project filter and the other filters hide a group with nothing to show, and a line names the ones hidden by search, Priority or Blocked; the Initiative filter shows its group alone, open. A card reorders within its column inside its group; a drop anywhere else is refused with a toast.

About the mock

Its data follows Task and Initiative in packages/contracts; the search here matches titles only, standing in for the service's. Drag and drop, the switch, folds, filters, Saved views and the Project filter all work. It uses the Geist fonts only where they are installed, and makes no network requests.
