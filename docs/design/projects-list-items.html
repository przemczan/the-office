The item

Only the inside of a large item changes; its size, hover and active colours stay those of section-layout.css.

32 12 52 260
Item The whole item a link, 52px tall, padding 0 12px, gap 12px, radius 8px. Hover --control-hover, open --surface-selected, as today.
Mark The Project's mark at size xl, 32px square: its Icon when it holds one, else its letters on its colour. Never the Logo.
Name 500 14px/1.3, --fg-1, one line, ending in an ellipsis; the full name in the item's tooltip when cut.
Line under it --type-caption (12px), --fg-3, one line, ending in an ellipsis; 1px under the name. The two lines sit centred against the mark.
Where it lives Its repository as owner/repository from its Repository URL; for a Project added by folder, the folder's name. Then · and its base branch.
Onboarding While its latest Onboard Task is in flight: a 6px dot and Onboarding in --info-fg (500), first on the line, then · and where it lives.
Trailing Nothing at the item's end: the name takes the whole width.

Spec wording

The Project item section of docs/spec/screens/projects.md would read:

## Project item

- The whole item is a link to the Project's page, about 52px tall.
- At its start, the Project's mark at 32px: its Icon, else its letters on its colour ([project-page.md](project-page.md#appearance)). The Logo shows only on the Project's page.
- Beside the mark, two lines, each one line ending in an ellipsis:
  - the Project's name, its whole name in the item's tooltip;
  - where it lives: its repository as owner/repository from its Repository URL, else its folder's name, then “·” and its base branch.
- While the Project's latest Onboard Task is in flight, the second line starts with **Onboarding** (info, with a dot) ([2.1.1](../2.1.1-projects.md#onboarding-a-project)). An onboarded Project shows nothing more; its page says it.
- The open Project's item is marked active on every section and sub-address of its page.
- A Project the Project filter hides still opens beside the list, from a link or an address, with no item marked.

And in project-page.md, Appearance: the Logo line reads “Stands for the Project on its page, in place of the mark.” and the “In the Projects list” sample draws this item.

For you to decide at the review

  1. 1. The Logo leaves the list. Fine? Recommended: yes, the mark (or the Icon) in the list, the Logo on the Project's page. Otherwise the framed alternative above.
  2. 2. “Onboarded” is dropped from the list. Fine? Recommended: yes, only “Onboarding” shows while it runs. Otherwise “Onboarded” shows in green in the same place, for every onboarded Project.
  3. 3. The line under the name: where it lives and the base branch? Recommended: yes. It tells apart two Projects of one name and costs no height. The other choice is the name alone on one line.
