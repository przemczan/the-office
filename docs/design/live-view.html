The figures

Each figure is generated from its Role key with a seeded random: build, height, skin, hair style and colour, top and accessory. Its clothes are the person's Avatar tint, worked out from their name as Avatar does (the top in the tone's -700, its trim in -100), so a figure and its Avatar match. The same key always gives the same figure: clears every texture and draws the sheet afresh.

Layered, so recolouring is a palette swap: body, then clothes, then hair, then the accessory, each a set of palette indexes. The same grids in the six Avatar tints:

How the floor grows

The floor is laid out from the people filling a Role, in three groups of pods (front desk, Consultants, everyone else), each in hire order. A new hire takes the first free desk of their group; when the group's pods are full, a new pod opens at the floor's right end, so nobody moves while you watch. The dashed desk is where the next hire who isn't a Consultant would sit.
