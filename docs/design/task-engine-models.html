A Task's models and efforts: the Runs on list

The switch "Override models and efforts" puts a Model and an Effort select on each row of "Runs on". As built, they are squeezed into the right half of a two-column row, each under its own long label, so the list turns ragged. This design gives each person one tidy block: who, what they work with, where it comes from, then the two selects side by side. Words, behaviour and saving stay as they are; only the layout and the selects' labels change.

Before: as built

The record column at about 460px. The selects sit in the right half only, labelled "Model for Pam" and "Effort for Pam", so they wrap one under another, the names float in the middle of tall rows, and what each person works with is said twice, far apart.

What to build

One person's block

Line 1
The person's avatar (PersonAvatar, small, as the Project page's per-person table) and name on the left, cut with an ellipsis; what they work with, "<model> · <effort>", on the right, never cut.
Line 2
Where it comes from, then each "<effort> on <Stage>" in the info colour, as captions joined by " · ", wrapping, under the name.
Selects
Only while the switch is on: Model and Effort, small size, two equal columns under the name, at most 440px wide together. One column when the list is 400px wide or less (a container query on the list).

Labels

  • The selects read "Model" and "Effort", not "Model for Pam": each person's block is a group named by the person (role="group" with the name), the way the Project page's settings window names each engine type's pair.
  • A select on "Unchanged · <value>" reads the same as today; nothing about its options changes.
  • An engine type that offers no efforts shows the Model select alone, in the first column.

Unchanged

  • Every word, toast and refusal; the switches; saving at once; the selects disabled while a change saves.
  • The read-only fold of a Done or Dropped Task, and the Initiative page's Engine fold with its "Its Tasks" list.
  • The fold's 72ch width cap and the divider above the second switch.

Where

  • Tests that find the selects by "Model for <name>": work-engine-block.test.tsx and e2e/work-engine.e2e.ts look inside the person's group instead.
