# 28 · The phone: HTTPS on the LAN, the PIN, the installed app, phone views and push

Status: postponed (by the user on 2026-10-29, during step 0; Q7 still open)

## Goal

The user reaches The Office from their phone on the LAN: over HTTPS from a certificate authority The Office makes and the phone trusts once, behind a PIN the phone remembers, installed as an app. At phone width The Office shows one column with bottom tabs (Now · People · Work · More): Needs you, passing Gates with their screenshots, Running, chatting, a Task's summary and the fire alarm. Notifications routed to the Phone column reach the phone as Web Push, through the `PhonePush` port task 27 built (the at-desk hold, the away delay and quiet hours already apply), and open there on the LAN. The Phone card in Settings › Notifications sets it up, with "Set it up with <Devops>" and "Send a test notification". "Alarm lifted with work waiting" is raised on the devices that did not give the all clear. Spec [3](../spec/3-capabilities-by-tier.md) (tier 2, Phone), [4](../spec/4-ux-direction.md) (Phone), [2.7](../spec/2.7-notifications.md) (channels, the phone in task 27 and Web Push in task 28, the grid's rows), [5.1](../spec/5.1-shape.md) (the LAN with a PIN and HTTPS; the Host and Origin checks); tickets [20](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/20-notifications.md), [12](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/12-architecture.md) (LAN access), [11](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/11-surfaces.md) (Phone), [15](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/15-fire-alarm.md) (on the phone), [18](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/18-people-mockup.md) item 7, [19](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/19-work-mockup.md) items 10 and 12; coverage row for task 28 in [coverage.md](coverage.md).

Check: service tests of sign-in, lockout, sign-out and the guard on HTTP and WebSocket; Playwright of the PIN page over HTTPS.
- [ ] The installed app: a web app manifest, icons and a service worker served by the service (not swallowed by the page fallback); the app installs on the phone and opens on the LAN; a tap off the LAN shows the service worker's own "Open The Office on your home network" page. - [ ] Web Push: VAPID keys made once and kept (Q8); a signed-in phone subscribes from the installed app; `WebPushPhonePush` sends each Notification the delivery rules give the phone (the person, the line, the Task and Project; opening what it points to), drops a subscription the push service answers 404 or 410; the Phone column and card show the phone set up; "Send a test notification" goes to every channel set up; handled Notifications are cleared on the phone as Q9 settles; "Alarm lifted with work waiting" is raised on the devices that did not give the all clear. Check: Playwright at a phone viewport.
- [ ] `npm run verify` and `npm run verify:linux` pass; the live check on the user's phone (trust the certificate, sign in with the PIN, install, a Needs you push arriving away from the desk and opening on the LAN, a Gate passed and the alarm sounded and cleared from the phone); the user's look at the Phone card and the phone views. Check: the commands; the live check; the user's word.

- At 720 px the shell has one column but no bottom tabs; `apps/web` has no `public/`, manifest or service worker.
- Open questions, most exposure first (each with the recommended answer; all but Q7 decided, below):
  1. Who makes the certificate: (a) The Office makes its own authority, name-constrained to the LAN's private ranges and names, its key never leaving the Office data directory (rec); (b) Devops installs mkcert (an install under the Hard switch; its authority is not constrained); (c) a self-signed server certificate, no authority. Any authority the phone trusts could vouch for other sites, hence the constraint. Also where it lives: (a) a new module, Access (the LAN switch, the certificate, the PIN, signed-in phones), amending spec 5.3's list (rec); (b) the platform layer for the certificate and guard, the phones in Notifications.
  2. The LAN switch: (a) off until the user turns it on in the Phone card, which needs a PIN set first (rec); (b) on whenever a PIN is set; (c) always on.
  3. The PIN: (a) 6 digits or more, set in the Phone card, 5 wrong tries lock sign-in for 15 minutes, localhost needs none (rec); (b) the same with a passphrase; (c) a PIN at localhost too.
  4. Staying signed in: (a) until the phone is signed out from the Phone card, a PIN change signing every phone out (rec); (b) 30 days; (c) each time the app opens.
  5. The authority's certificate before HTTPS is trusted: (a) a plain-HTTP download of that file only, on the LAN, with its fingerprint shown at the desk and on the phone (rec); (b) accept the browser's warning once and download over HTTPS; (c) the user copies the file to the phone themselves.
  6. The phone's address: (a) the workstation's name (`<name>.local`) and its current LAN addresses in the certificate, the server certificate issued again when they change, the installed app on the name (rec); (b) one fixed address the user reserves in the router; (c) the address alone.
  7. The phone: iPhone (push only in the installed app; the authority needs "full trust" in Settings) or Android (Chrome trusts a user authority): the guide and the live check follow the user's phone.
  8. VAPID keys: (a) in the Office data directory beside the authority's key (rec); (b) in the database.
  9. Clearing on the phone. Spec 2.7 says handling clears a Notification on every channel, but Web Push may not send a push that shows nothing (iOS revokes a subscription that does): (a) the service worker closes handled ones whenever it wakes, at the next push or when the app opens (rec; amends 2.7); (b) they clear only when tapped.
  10. Glossary terms (none exist for these): **PIN** (what a phone signs in with on the LAN), **Phone** (a device signed in with the PIN, listed in the Phone card), **The Office's certificate** (the authority the phone trusts once). Proposed; accept or rename.
  11. "Alarm lifted with work waiting": (a) raised when the all clear is given on one device, on the others, while any Task stays held or Needs you holds anything (rec); (b) raised on the others at every all clear.
  12. Activity on the phone: (a) only the desk's activity holds phone pushes and marks where Since you left begins (rec); (b) any device's activity counts, so using the phone holds its pushes too.
  13. "Service down" goes to the phone by ticket 20's defaults, but the service is what pushes: (a) the phone shows it only in the app, as the desktop browser does today (rec); (b) leave the cell and say so in its hint.
