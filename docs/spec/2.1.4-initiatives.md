# 2.1.4 Initiatives

Covers: Initiative, Initiative states, shaping, creating an Initiative, the CTO's changes, Decisions in the shaping Thread, the Initiative's Project, outcome, done, Initiative's Pipeline, Initiative's engine override, Publishing Tasks, Plan the Initiative, Start Ready Tasks, Pause the Initiative, test instances of an Initiative, Office actions on an Initiative, the Initiatives tab.

## Initiative

- Office-level; spans Projects. Each of its Tasks is in one Project, and an Initiative spans the Projects of its Tasks (nothing else is stored).
- It is shaped from an Idea ([2.1.2](2.1.2-ideas-and-attachments.md#idea)), or created with `create_initiative` ([Creating an Initiative](#creating-an-initiative)).
- Order inside it is dependencies plus manual order.
- Each Project it spans may have a Pipeline of its own for it, the **Initiative's Pipeline** in that Project: there it works on the **Initiative's branch** ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md#defaults)), and that Project's **Publishing Task** publishes the whole change ([An Initiative's Pipeline](#an-initiatives-pipeline), [Publishing Tasks](#publishing-tasks)).
- Its Tasks' states, joining and leaving: [2.1.3](2.1.3-tasks.md#joining-and-leaving-an-initiative).

## Initiative states

- **Shaping** (with the CTO), **active**, **done** (all Tasks done), **dropped**.
- Its Tasks wait in Shaping until Confirm ([2.1.3](2.1.3-tasks.md#tasks-of-an-initiative-still-being-shaped)).
- **Back to Shaping** moves an active one back to shaping ([Back to Shaping](#back-to-shaping)).
- Done: [An Initiative is done](#an-initiative-is-done). Archive: [2.1.3](2.1.3-tasks.md#archive).

## Plan the Initiative

- An active Initiative's page has a **Plan the Initiative** action with the same **Auto mode** switch as Make a plan ([2.1.5](2.1.5-planning-and-start.md#auto-mode)), starting at the remembered default; the choice becomes the default.
- It opens one planning Thread with the Planner on the Initiative:
  - one session, reading every Project's base branch (the Initiative's branch in each Project where it has a Pipeline: [2.4](2.4-conversations.md)) and changing no file, as the CTO's shaping Thread does.
- The Planner plans the Initiative's Backlog Tasks in dependency order, where only blockers inside the Initiative count: a Task's turn comes once each of them is marked planned, Ready or beyond, or Dropped.
- Where the engine has subagents, the Planner hands each Task's research and drafting to one and plans the Tasks whose blockers are planned in parallel; without them it plans them one after another.
- As each Task's turn comes, the Planner moves it from Backlog to Planning with the `start_planning` Office tool.
  - It works in any of the Initiative's planning Threads, the latest or an earlier one, in that Thread's auto mode.
  - The Task then belongs to the Thread that moved it: a write to it from another planning Thread is refused with "<title> is being planned in another planning Thread: write there, or wait until it is marked planned."
  - Refused with the reason for a Task outside the Initiative or not in Backlog, while a blocker inside the Initiative isn't planned yet, and from a Thread that isn't one of the Initiative's planning Threads.
- The Planner writes the Task's Plan with its files to change, Definition of Done, Pipeline and Tags itself, with the planning tools naming the Task by its id, and marks it planned with `mark_planned`.
  - Auto mode then works as for a single Task; without it the Task waits in Planning for the user's Confirm.
- The Initiative's Thread counts as the planning Thread of each Task planned in it:
  - the Task's Planner's tab links to it;
  - no Planner's Thread opens on the Task meanwhile;
  - the Task has no worktree until Start makes one.
- The page links to the Thread and shows each Task's progress (Waiting, Planning, Planned, Ready), with a count of the Tasks planned.
- Each planning Thread keeps its own auto mode, who asked for it and when it was opened; the latest planning Thread is the one opened last.
- Its Brief names the Initiative's other planning Threads ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md#an-initiatives-planning-threads-brief)).
- The action is refused with the reason when:
  - the Initiative isn't active;
  - nobody holds Plans;
  - the Initiative's latest planning Thread has a turn running: "<Planner> is still planning in the planning Thread: write there, or wait until they finish.";
  - none of its Tasks is in Backlog;
  - one of its Tasks is still being planned in an earlier planning Thread (in Planning there, not yet marked planned).
- Only a running turn counts: a planning Thread waiting for the user, or for a Decision's answer, isn't working.

## Decisions in the planning Thread

- The Planner opens a Decision, the form of [2.4](2.4-conversations.md), with `open_decision` naming one of the Initiative's Tasks by its `taskId`: one per Task, several open at once.
- One naming a Task outside the Initiative is refused with the reason.
- One naming no Task is about the Initiative as a whole, one at a time, and shows in the Thread and under Needs you with the Initiative's title.
- A Task's Decision card shows:
  - in the Thread, naming its Task;
  - on that Task's page in Needs you, linking to this Thread;
  - in Mark Ready's checklist, where it keeps the Task from Ready until answered.
- It is answered in any of these places.
- A Decision on a Task stays in the planning Thread that owns the Task, and its answer resumes that Thread, the latest or an earlier one.
- While a Decision on a Task is open, that Task isn't marked planned and the Tasks of the Initiative that wait for it are refused `start_planning`, as their blocker isn't planned.
- The Planner plans the other Tasks and ends its turn only when every Task left waits for an answer, or all are planned.
- An answer given while the Planner waits resumes the session at once.
- An answer given while it works is recorded at once, so the Decision is answered and the Task is free for Ready, and reaches the session when its turn ends, every waiting answer in one message, each naming its Task.
- If that message never reaches a session, those Decisions open again.

## Rewrites in the planning Thread

- When an answer or a later decision changes Tasks already planned, the Planner rewrites their Plan, Definition of Done or Pipeline from any of the Initiative's planning Threads.
- A Task in Planning marked planned, in any planning Thread of the Initiative, is rewritten and marked planned again.
- The rewriting Thread becomes the Task's owner, and the Task takes that Thread's auto mode.
- A Task in Planning not yet marked planned belongs to its own Thread alone: a rewrite from another planning Thread is refused with the reason.
- A Ready Task goes back to Planning in this Thread in one move, as on a changing Save ([2.1.5](2.1.5-planning-and-start.md#changing-a-ready-task)), the rewritten part Not confirmed, and is marked planned again.
  - Auto mode then accepts it and marks it Ready again; without it, it waits for the user's Confirm.
- Writing any part of a Task marked planned clears its marked planned in every Thread; Tags never move a Task.
- A Task that has started (In progress, Waiting on you, Paused, Done), is Dropped or is being planned in its own planning Thread is never rewritten: the write is refused with the reason, and the Planner opens a Decision on that Task instead, saying what changed and what it recommends.

## Plan with… on an Initiative

- Beside Plan the Initiative, as a Task has beside Make a plan, the page offers **Plan with <Planner>**; the Planner's tab on the Initiative offers it too while it has no Thread there.
- It opens a new planning Thread on the Initiative where the Planner waits for the user's first message: no session starts and no model is called until the user writes there how to plan it (order, depth, what to ask, subagents or not).
- The Planner's first turn starts from that message, with the Initiative's planning Brief saying the user opened the Thread to plan with them; their messages say how to plan and the Planner follows them where they differ from the default way, asking in the Thread what is unclear.
- It plans with auto mode off and leaves the remembered default unchanged.
- It is offered and refused as Plan the Initiative is, with the same reasons.
- Its Thread becomes the Initiative's latest planning Thread like any other: `start_planning`, Decisions, rewrites and the page's progress work the same.
- A restart of The Office wakes it only once a Task is being planned there.

## Test instances of an Initiative

- An Initiative's page launches a test instance of a Project's test target on the Initiative's branch in that Project, at its tip, fetched first ([2.12](2.12-test-instances.md#test-instance)).
- It is offered for each Project the Initiative spans where it has a branch, one row per test target, while the Initiative is active.
- Several instances of one target may exist, on newer commits; Test instances at once limits how many are Building, Installing or Running ([2.12](2.12-test-instances.md#test-instances-at-once)).
- Its instances stop when it is Done or Dropped, and its Build to install files are removed 7 days later ([2.12](2.12-test-instances.md#kept-build-to-install-files)).

## Defaults

### Shaping without a CTO

- An Idea is shaped into an Initiative whether or not anyone holds Shapes Initiatives ([2.2](2.2-people.md#defaults), "Duties").
- With a holder, the shaping Thread opens at once and they start on it.
- While nobody holds it, the user writes the Initiative by hand, and its Thread opens when they start it once someone holds Shapes Initiatives.

### Creating an Initiative

- The holders of Shapes Initiatives and Default person to talk to, and no other Role, have `create_initiative` in every Thread the user talks to them in ([6](6-permissions-and-safety.md#a-persons-office-tools)), and use it only when the user asks for an Initiative or agrees to one.
- It takes a title and an outcome, the outcome naming the Projects meant.
- No Projects are stored, as an Initiative spans its Tasks' Projects; the CTO adds the Tasks while shaping.
- The Initiative lands in shaping, created by that person, with no origin Idea, and waits for the user's Confirm on its page like one shaped from an Idea.
- Its shaping Thread opens as in [Shaping without a CTO](#shaping-without-a-cto), with or without a CTO; its opening has the CTO check the outcome with the user, then agree on the Projects and the Tasks.
- Its page shows no Origin.

### The CTO's changes to an Initiative

- While it is shaping, the CTO writes the outcome, Tasks and dependencies straight onto it, and the user confirms the whole once.
- Once it is active, each change the CTO wants is a Proposal the user applies.
- Its Tasks' dependencies and which Tasks it has are the exception: those Office actions change an active Initiative straight away too, as the user's own click does, gated by the person's Role authority.
  - The CTO's card says to ask the user before changing an active Initiative, so they ask with a Decision first, opened in the shaping Thread.
- The title and Description of one of its Tasks are among these changes, and both change only until that Task starts.
- So is the Initiative's Pipeline in each of its Projects ([An Initiative's Pipeline](#an-initiatives-pipeline)).

### Decisions in the shaping Thread

- Whoever holds Shapes Initiatives asks the user with a Decision, the form of [2.4](2.4-conversations.md), opened in the Initiative's shaping Thread on no Task, while it is shaping or active, one at a time.
- It shows in that Thread and under Needs you with the Initiative's title, and its answer comes as the Thread's next message.

### An Initiative starts in its Idea's Project

- An Initiative shaped from an Idea captured in a Project starts in that Project.
- The CTO's shaping Brief says so, and the CTO asks only whether it reaches other Projects too, not which Project it is for.
- Add a Task on its page chooses that Project until the Initiative has Tasks.
- Nothing new is stored: an Initiative still spans the Projects of its Tasks.

### The user writes the outcome

- With or without a CTO, the user edits an Initiative's outcome on its page while it is shaping.
- The outcome can mention a Task, Initiative, open Decision or Idea, picked with `@` as in a Task's Description ([2.1.3](2.1.3-tasks.md#description)); it is saved as a Markdown link and shown as the record's chip.
- Once it is active, the outcome changes only by a Proposal the user applies, as the CTO's other changes do.
- Whoever creates Initiatives in a Thread writes it with `write_initiative_outcome` while it is shaping ([Office actions on an Initiative](#office-actions-on-an-initiative)).
- Each edit of the outcome is kept in the Initiative's History, naming who ([2.1.3 History](2.1.3-tasks.md#history)).

### An Initiative is done

- It is done once it is active and every one of its Tasks that isn't Dropped is Done, at least one of them.
- A shaping one waits for Confirm, which makes it done at once when its Tasks already are.
- Its Publishing Tasks, in its Projects with a Pipeline, count among its Tasks, so it is done only once they are Done too.
- Dropping an Initiative, shaping or active, drops every one of its Tasks not yet Done or Dropped with it:
  - those in Shaping, Backlog, Planning, Ready (queued too), In progress, Waiting on you and Paused, its Publishing Tasks included;
  - each as that Task's own Drop would, so a queued one leaves the queue and a started one has its running Stage stopped;
  - each change is recorded as made by whoever Dropped the Initiative.
- Its Done and Dropped Tasks stay as they are.
- An Agent Drops one with `drop_initiative` only while none of its Tasks has started; otherwise it is refused, "\"<name>\" has a Task that has started, \"<task>\", so only the user Drops it: ask them to."
- The user's Drop goes through whatever its Tasks' states.
- After a Drop no Publishing Task is added, and its Initiative's branches are kept until deleted by hand; Delete branches once merged never deletes a Dropped Initiative's ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md#deleting-merged-branches)).
- Once Done, its branch in each Project is deleted by hand, or by Delete branches once merged once everything on it is in the base or its Publishing Task's merged Publish ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md#deleting-merged-branches)).

### An Initiative's Pipeline

- **Each Project the Initiative spans has a Pipeline of its own, or None**, chosen while shaping from the office-wide Pipelines and that Project's own Project Pipelines, as a Task of that Project chooses.
  - A Project Pipeline of another Project is refused.
- It is chosen by the user on the Initiative page or by the CTO with an Office tool, each naming the Project, both straight onto the Initiative.
- It starts as **None** in each Project; no Project's default applies.
- Confirm fixes, for each Project it spans then, whether it has one.
- A Project with None publishes its Tasks one by one, each by its own Pipeline.
- The Initiative's **Short name** stays one for the whole Initiative, made once any Project has a Pipeline ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md#defaults)).
- A Project the Initiative comes to span after Confirm, by a Task added or moved there, starts with None, and gets a Pipeline only by the CTO's Proposal.
- Once the Initiative is active, which Pipeline a Project has changes by the CTO's Proposal, naming the Project, applied by the user, or by the user on the Initiative page, in the Pipeline picker of each Project already on an Initiative's branch.
- Once active, the on/off switch and the branch name are fixed at Confirm (go Back to Shaping to change them), the Short name can't change, and a Project whose Publishing Task has started is read-only.
- The user's change once active follows the rules and effects of an applied Proposal below.
- Applying a Proposal is refused:
  - once that Project's Publishing Task has started ("the Pipeline starts");
  - when that Project lacks a command the Pipeline runs ([2.3](2.3-pipelines.md#defaults));
  - for another Project's Project Pipeline;
  - when it would change whether that Project has a Pipeline, which Confirm fixed: a Proposal never proposes None, nor a Pipeline for a Project that had None at Confirm.
- Another Project's Publishing Task doesn't block it.
- A Proposal giving a Project spanned after Confirm its first Pipeline is allowed as long as that Project has no Publishing Task that started, and makes the Initiative's branch there.
- An applied Proposal also changes the Pipeline of that Project's Publishing Task not yet started, unless the user edited its Stages for that Task.
- In each Project the Initiative names an office-wide Pipeline or a Project Pipeline, as a Task that hasn't started does, so an edit to it changes it; that Project's Publishing Task takes it as its own Pipeline under a Task's rules, a Project Pipeline as it stands when the Task starts.
- An edit of an office-wide Pipeline never reaches a Project Pipeline copied from it ([2.3.5](2.3.5-library.md#project-pipelines)).
- The Initiative's other Tasks keep Pipelines of their own: the Initiative's Pipeline in a Project runs only in that Project's Publishing Task.
  - Those Tasks, in a Project with a Pipeline or with None, choose from their own Project's Project Pipelines and the office-wide ones, like any Task.

### An Initiative's engine

- An Initiative can force one engine, its **engine override**, turned on with the switch "Override the engine", which its shaping and planning Threads run on, and every Task in it with no engine override of its own.
- It names an engine only, no model: each person still works with their model and effort for its engine type ([5.4.5](5.4.5-engines-and-cascade.md#the-model-and-effort-cascade)).
- It uses no Stand-in Engine: when its usage window is used up, the Tasks it gives the engine wait or pause until the reset ([5.4.6](5.4.6-stand-in-engines-and-usage-windows.md#a-forced-engine-has-no-stand-ins)).
- It is set and cleared on the Initiative page until the Initiative is Done or Dropped, and applies from each session's next start; a Done or Dropped Initiative keeps it as history.
- Where it stands in the engine cascade, below a Task's engine override, and the check a save passes over its open Tasks: [5.4.5](5.4.5-engines-and-cascade.md#the-engine-cascade).

### Back to Shaping

- **Back to Shaping** undoes Confirm: a user action on an active Initiative's page; Agents get no tool for it.
- It is refused, each with its reason, when:
  - the Initiative isn't active;
  - any of its Tasks has started (In progress, Waiting on you, Paused or Done);
  - any of its Tasks has a worktree;
  - any of its Tasks is being planned right now (in Planning, not yet marked planned, in any planning Thread);
  - it has a Publishing Task;
  - a branch of the Initiative, locally or on the remote, holds commits its freshly fetched base branch doesn't have, naming that Project.
- It does, in one step:
  - the Initiative goes from active to shaping, and a Paused mark is cleared;
  - every Task in Backlog, Planning or Ready, queued ones too, moves to Shaping: a queued Task leaves the queue, its Plan, Definition of Done and Pipeline are kept but Not confirmed (the Pipeline back as proposed), and it is no longer marked planned in any Thread;
  - each move is recorded as the user's; Dropped Tasks stay Dropped.
- The next Confirm moves these Tasks to Backlog as usual, and they are planned again.
- Before the hold, in each Project with a branch, The Office checks it holds nothing the base lacks and removes it, locally and on the remote if it was pushed there ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md#the-initiatives-branch)); when one holds commits, it is refused naming the Project, and any branch already removed is made again.
- The per-Project Pipelines stay as chosen, but nothing is fixed any more: the Pipelines editor's switch, branch name and Short name are editable again, and the next Confirm makes the branches again under the Short name as it is then.
- Its planning Threads are kept; no Task is planned in them while it is shaping, as Tasks in Shaping aren't planned.
- Screen: [Initiative page](screens/initiative-page.md#back-to-shaping).

### Publishing Tasks

- Only a Project where the Initiative has a Pipeline gets one, following that Project's Pipeline; a Project with None gets none.
- They are added in the same step that makes the last of the Initiative's other Tasks Done or Dropped, and only while it is active.
- A Project with a Pipeline gets one when its Initiative's branch holds commits its last Done Publishing Task didn't publish (or, with none yet, commits the base branch doesn't have) and it has no unfinished Publishing Task.
  - So a Project whose Tasks were all Dropped gets none, and a dropped Publishing Task isn't added again until another of the Initiative's Tasks ends.
- It is titled "<Initiative> → <Project>", in the Initiative and that Project, and its Description is the Initiative's outcome.
- Its Plan is The Office's own words: the Initiative's branch, the base branch, the publishing mode and the Initiative's Done Tasks in that Project.
- Its Definition of Done is every statement of those Tasks plus "The whole change is in <base branch> by <publishing mode>", and its Files to change are the union of theirs.
- It is added **Ready**, its parts confirmed by The Office as the Onboard Task's are, and its state change records it; it never enters Planning.
- The user starts it, or Start Ready Tasks does; it isn't started on its own.
- It works on the Initiative's branch itself and publishes it into the base branch ([2.3](2.3.4-publish.md#a-publishing-tasks-publish), "A Publishing Task's Publish").
- A fix that needs new work is a new Task in the Initiative, added by the user or by the CTO's Proposal; while its Project's Publishing Task isn't Done or Dropped, The Office makes the new Task a blocker of it, and a reopened Dropped Task of the Initiative becomes its blocker again ([Reopen](2.1.3-tasks.md#reopen)).
- A Publishing Task with an unfinished blocker queues like any blocked Task; once started, it waits before its next Stage, with the blocker as the reason.
- It can't be moved to another Project ([Moving a Task](2.1.3-tasks.md#moving-a-task)).

### Start Ready Tasks

- On an active Initiative's page with at least one of its Tasks Ready, one action starts every Ready Task of the Initiative through the normal Start, so blockers, the queue, the Office-wide limit, Project limits, each person's Task limit, usage caps, a scheduled update and the fire alarm apply as they do to each Start ([2.1.5](2.1.5-planning-and-start.md#a-queue-for-start)).
- It is refused, with the reason, for an Initiative that isn't active or has no Ready Task.
- A blocker inside the Initiative starts before the Tasks that wait for it, so its queue place is ahead of theirs; otherwise the board's order applies (priority, then manual order).
- A Task that would change the same files as a Task already started, including one started earlier in the same run, is queued behind it without the user being asked ([Overlaps at Start](2.1.5-planning-and-start.md#overlaps-at-start)).
- A Task that has to wait is queued, and the queue starts it later as it does any other.
- A Task Start refuses (a vacant Role, a Pipeline no longer in the library) is listed with its reasons, and the rest still start or queue.
- Afterwards the page shows, until closed or reloaded, what started, what was queued with its place and reasons, and what was refused with its reasons, each Task linked to its page; nothing about the run is kept on the Initiative.
- The queue's release doesn't weigh Overlaps, so two Tasks of one run both queued for a limit may later start together, as two queued by hand can.

### Pause the Initiative

- Nothing of a paused Initiative runs or starts until it is resumed.
- On an active Initiative's page, **Pause the Initiative** marks it Paused, beside its state, as Archive marks a Task.
- Each of its Tasks In progress or Waiting on you is paused as the user's own Pause pauses it: the running Stage is held once the Agent's current turn ends, and its edits are kept.
- None of its Tasks starts while it is paused: a Start, Start Ready Tasks or a turn in the queue waits with "The Initiative is paused.", so the Task is queued, and so is a Task added to it meanwhile.
- **Resume the Initiative** clears the mark, resumes exactly the Tasks its Pause paused, and releases the queue.
  - A Task the user had paused themselves before stays Paused, as the fire alarm leaves it ([2.8](2.8-fire-alarm.md)).
- Pause is offered only while the Initiative is active and not paused, Resume only while it is paused; otherwise each is refused with the reason.
- While it is paused, Resume on one of its Tasks is refused with "The Initiative is paused: resume it to go on.", and Stop and Drop still work on a single Task.
- Dropping a paused Initiative clears the mark and drops the Tasks its Pause paused with its other open Tasks ([An Initiative is done](#an-initiative-is-done)); its move to Done, once its paused Tasks were dropped and the rest are Done, clears the mark too.
- Pausing raises no "Task paused" Notification and no Outcome for each Task, as the user paused them all at once.
- The Initiative shows Paused on its page, its card in the Initiatives tab and its group on the Work board.
- Planning goes on while it is paused: its planning Thread isn't held.
- It isn't offered for a shaping Initiative, which has no started Tasks, nor from the Work board or an Initiative's card, nor to Agents.

### Office actions on an Initiative

- Whoever creates Initiatives in a Thread (`create_initiative`) changes them with the Office actions an Initiative's creators cover ([5.4.1](5.4.1-engine-kinds.md#office-actions)), each the same route as the user's click:
  - `write_initiative_outcome`, while it is shaping;
  - `drop_initiative` ([An Initiative is done](#an-initiative-is-done));
  - `archive_initiative` and `restore_initiative` ([Archive](2.1.3-tasks.md#archive)).
- A person's Role authority says when they use them: their own mistake or what the user clarified, alone; an Initiative somebody else created, after asking the user ([2.2](2.2-people.md#authority-and-hard-switches)).

### The Initiatives tab is a board

- Design: [its design](../design/initiatives-tab.html).
- Columns: Shaping, Active and Done, each newest first but Done, the one that ended last on top, with Dropped folded at its foot.
- Its search (of names and outcomes) and Waiting on you are kept in the address; the Dropped fold is remembered in this browser.
- Every column always shows, with its count and a line saying why it is empty.
- The Project filter narrows the board and the archive.
- The Archived tab shows the archived Tasks or, switched, the archived Initiatives: a search, a Done / Dropped / All filter with counts, the one that ended last on top under a line per month, 25 to a page, all kept in the address ([Archive](2.1.3-tasks.md#archive)).
