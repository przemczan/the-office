# 2.11.1 The Stats page

Covers: what Stats shows, its names, its place on the rail, the tabs, the Project filter, Office-wide blocks, this week.

## What Stats shows

- The UI spec is [docs/design/stats.html](../design/stats.html).
- The telemetry it reads and the Grafana it reads it from are in [5.2](5.2-stack.md); the module that owns it is in [5.3](5.3-modules.md#defaults).
- **Stats** shows how The Office performs, in two tabs.
- **Tokens and cost** shows what The Office spends in tokens and in the engines' Usage windows, so the user can see what costs the most and make it cheaper ([2.11.2](2.11.2-tokens-and-cost.md)).
- **System health** shows how well The Office itself runs, now and over time: how long its work waits, its CPU and memory, its requests and its database queries, and what is running now ([2.11.3](2.11.3-system-health.md)).
- System health lets the user spot a slow spell and find the route or query behind it.

## Names

- Names are in the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md): **Stats**, **Tokens and cost**, **Basic stats**, **Full stats**, **System health**, **Health reading**, **Statement shape**, **Grafana read address**, **Read token** and **Stage retry**.
- Never "dashboard" or "analytics" for Stats.
- Never "monitoring" or "performance" for System health.
- Never "rerun" or "attempt" for a Stage retry.

## Place

- Stats is a surface at `/stats`, titled "Stats", on the rail between Projects and the Live view; the Live view stays last.
- Its icon is `chart-column`.
- It stays one item: the tabs are inside the page.

## The tabs

- packages/ui's `Tabs` sit under the page's head, as Work's do, each tab going to its address.
- **Tokens and cost** is at `/stats`, so earlier links keep working.
- **System health** is at `/stats/system-health`.
- The top bar's crumb names the tab ("Stats › System health").
- Each tab's range picker sits at the right end of the tabs' row; on a narrow page it wraps under the tabs, as a `Select`.

## The Project filter

- It narrows only what belongs to a Task, as `day_tokens` and the Usage windows have no Project.
- In the basic stats it narrows the top Tasks, Tasks done and Stage retries.
- In the full stats it narrows tokens and estimated cost over time (by `office.project`), the most expensive Tasks and Initiatives, and the lane of what was running.

## Office-wide blocks

- They say "Office-wide" in their head, whatever the filter.
- They are tokens per day, the Usage windows now, the Usage windows over time, and every block of System health.
- While a filter is set, the badge's `Tooltip` says "The Project filter doesn't narrow this: these figures belong to The Office as a whole."

## This week

- It starts on Monday at 00:00 on the service's clock: the office day (`officeDayOf`, [2.7](2.7-notifications.md#defaults), "Today's figures") that is this week's Monday.
- So "this week", "today" and tokens per day agree near midnight and across a change of clock.
- Its blocks say "since Mon 16 Nov".

## Defaults

### Tokens per day and the Usage windows are Office-wide

- They are labelled so.
- `day_tokens` has no Project, so the Project filter narrows only what belongs to a Task.

### "This week"

- It starts Monday 00:00 on the service's clock, the same clock as "today" (`officeDayOf`).

### System health is Office-wide

- It is labelled so: The Office's own performance belongs to no Project.
