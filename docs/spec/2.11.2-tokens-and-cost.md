# 2.11.2 Tokens and cost

Covers: the basic stats, the full stats, the states of Stats.

## Levels

- The **basic stats** are always there.
- They use only data The Office already keeps, so nothing new is collected for them.
- They show no cost anywhere: estimated cost isn't stored, only sent as telemetry.
- The **full stats** show once Grafana answers: the bundled Grafana running, or the user's own Grafana with its read address in Settings › Telemetry.
- The Office draws them itself from Grafana's data, with estimated cost by model, Role, Stage, Pipeline and Project.
- Each block says what would fill it when it is empty.
- The basic stats are read when the page opens and every minute while it is open.

## Basic stats

### Tokens per day

- It is Office-wide: the last 30 office days, ending today, from `day_tokens` (Notifications' day figures).
- Each day stacks its input, output and cache writes.
- Cache reads are shown but left out of the total, as every token count in The Office leaves them out ([2.7](2.7-notifications.md#defaults), "Today's figures"): they are drawn in a strip of their own under the chart, "Cache reads, not counted".
- A day's `Tooltip` gives all four kinds and its total.
- The head reads "12.4M tokens in 30 days · 410k today".
- A day with nothing shows as 0.

### The Tasks with the most tokens this week

- Up to 10, from the Stage runs started this week (`stage_runs.tokens`, counted as above), most first.
- Each row shows the Task's title, a link to its page, its Project's chip, its tokens and a bar against the first row's.
- Only Stage runs count, so a Task's other Threads (its planning, a person's own Thread on it) and Consultations aren't in it.
- A hint says so, and these figures aren't meant to match the full stats'.
- Empty: "No Stage has run this week yet."

### Usage windows now

- It is Office-wide: each engine that has Usage windows, in Settings › Engines' order, with its 5-hour and 7-day windows from `UsageWindowState`.
- Each window has a meter, how full it is and when it resets ("41% · resets 14:00", "88% · resets Mon 09:00"), amber from 90% and red when used up, as on the status strip.
- An engine whose windows aren't read yet shows "Not read yet".
- An engine without Usage windows (a Codex engine on an API key, a Copilot CLI engine, a Gemini CLI engine, the OpenAI-compatible API) isn't listed.
- When none has any: "None of your engines has Usage windows."

### This week

- **Tasks done:** the Tasks that moved to Done this week, each counted once (from `task_state_changes`).
- **Stage retries:** the Stage runs started this week whose Stage already had a run on the same Task, whenever that one ran.
- So a retry this week of a Stage first run last week counts.

### The message

- It is at the foot of the basic stats while there are no full stats, with its words by state ([States](#states)).
- "Turn on Grafana in Settings › Telemetry to see more", a link to Settings › Telemetry, and one line on what the full stats add: "Estimated cost by model, Role, Stage, Pipeline and Project, the most expensive Tasks and Initiatives, and how full the Usage windows were over time."

## Full stats

- The Office queries Grafana's data (its metrics and its logs, through Grafana's data source query API, [5.2](5.2-stack.md)) and draws the full stats itself, with the design system's charts.
- They are never Grafana panels embedded in the page, so they follow the palette, the Project filter and the [UI rules](4.1-ui-rules.md).
- Every chart has a `Tooltip` per point and "Show as a table", which gives its figures as a table.
- They are read when the page opens, when the range changes and every minute while it is open.

### The range

- 24 h, 7 d (the default) or 30 d, picked above the full stats and kept in the address.
- 24 h goes by the hour, 7 d by six hours and 30 d by the day (UTC).

### The coverage line

- It is under the range: "The full stats cover what The Office has sent Grafana since it started sending it."
- The basic stats still cover the time before.

### Tokens and estimated cost over time

- **Tokens or Estimated cost**, a switch.
- **Split by** model, Role, Stage, Pipeline or Project, from the attributes on `office.tokens` and `office.cost`.
- The chart stacks the five largest values and "Other".
- The legend gives each value's total in the range.
- The head reads "4.2M tokens · $38.10 estimated · 7 days".
- Tokens count as everywhere: cache reads are left out, and the `Tooltip` gives them.
- Work outside a Stage shows as "Not in a Stage".
- A Task's calls always carry its Pipeline and Project, in a Stage or not.
- A chat and an Initiative's Thread show as "No Pipeline" and "No Project".
- Estimated cost is in US dollars, always called "estimated" ([5.2](5.2-stack.md), "Estimated cost").

### The most expensive Tasks and Initiatives

- A Tasks or Initiatives switch shows the 10 with the most estimated cost in the range, from the `office.engine.call` records.
- Each row shows the title, a link to its page, its Project's chip (an Initiative shows each Project it spans), its estimated cost and tokens, its Stage runs and its Stage retries.
- Opening a row shows its **cost per Stage**: each Stage with its runs, "Run 1 · $1.20", "Run 2 · retry · $0.90".
- Each run, and each Task of an Initiative, names the models and engines its calls ran on, with the Stand-in Engine marked; the words are on the [Stats screen](screens/stats.md#the-most-expensive).
- The engine and model come from the `office.engine.call` records; the stand-in and the engine's name from the Stage run's record (`office.stage.run` is the run's number on its Task).
- The row's head says what the retries cost, "Retries: $4.10, 18%".
- A Task's calls outside a Stage (its planning, a person's own Thread on it) show as "Outside a Stage".
- An Initiative holds its Tasks and its own Threads, "The Initiative's Threads".
- The Project filter narrows the Tasks, and the Initiatives to those with a Task in the filter, each still with its whole totals.

### The Usage windows over time

- It is Office-wide.
- Each engine with Usage windows shows how full its 5-hour and 7-day windows were over the range, from `office.usage_window.used`.
- Lines mark 90% and 100%, and a reset shows as the line dropping.
- With several engines, a picker shows one at a time, the fullest first.
- Under the chart, on the same time axis, runs a **lane of what was running** on that engine, "What was running on <engine>".
- The lane has one bar per Agent Stage run, from Pipelines' Stage runs, one row per Task, in the Task's Project colour.
- The Project filter narrows the lane.
- A bar's `Tooltip` gives its times, the Task and the Stage; a press opens the Task.

### What stays of the basic stats

- The basic stats' Usage windows now and This week stay under the full stats.
- Tokens per day and the top Tasks give way to their full versions.

## States

- What Stats shows follows Settings › Telemetry and whether Grafana answers.
- Whatever goes wrong with Grafana, the basic stats stay.
- While Grafana starts, the full stats' loaders hold their place, so the page goes from them straight to the full stats ([screens/stats.md](screens/stats.md)).
- The three Basic states' words are the message at the foot of the basic stats, said once.
- Every other state's words go in an `Alert` above the stats, and there is no message at the foot.

| State | When | Its words | Its next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | Telemetry is Off. | "Turn on Grafana in Settings › Telemetry to see more." | The link to Settings › Telemetry. |
| **Basic**, your own endpoint | Your own endpoint, with no Grafana read address. | "Add your Grafana's read address in Settings › Telemetry to see more." | The link to Settings › Telemetry. |
| **Basic**, set by the environment | Any OTEL_* variable sets telemetry ([5.2](5.2-stack.md)), so The Office can't tell which Grafana to read. | "The environment sets telemetry, so Stats shows the basic stats only." | "Remove the OTEL_ variables to choose in Settings › Telemetry", with its link. |
| **Grafana starting** | Bundled Grafana is chosen and starting. | "Grafana is starting…", then "The first start pulls its image, which can take a few minutes." | None: the full stats show by themselves once it runs, read every few seconds while it starts. |
| **Couldn't start** | Bundled Grafana is chosen and couldn't start. | "The bundled Grafana couldn't start", then its problem as Settings › Telemetry says it. | The link to Settings › Telemetry, where Try again is. |
| **Unreachable** | Grafana is running or its read address is set, but a query fails to reach it: no connection, no answer within 10 seconds, or an answer that isn't Grafana's. | "Couldn't reach Grafana at http://127.0.0.1:3300", then the reason in plain words. | **Try again**, then the link to Settings › Telemetry. It is tried again every minute too. |
| **Read token rejected** | Your own Grafana answered 401 or 403. | "Grafana refused the read token", then "Grafana at <address> answered 401. Give a Viewer service account token in Settings › Telemetry." | The link to Settings › Telemetry. |
| **No data yet** | Grafana answers but holds nothing The Office sent. | "Grafana has no data from The Office yet. The full stats start with the next engine call." | None. The basic stats show as in Basic, with no message. |
| **Full** | Grafana answers with data. | The coverage line. | None. |

- **An engine without Usage windows** is left out of the Usage windows now and over time.
- With no engine that has any, both blocks say "None of your engines has Usage windows."
- **While the full stats load**, each full block shows its `BlockLoader` shape; the basic stats don't wait for them.
- **A query that fails partway**, such as the logs answering and the metrics not, shows the blocks that answered and Unreachable's words on the ones that didn't.

## Defaults

### Estimated cost

- It is priced from Settings › Engines › Model prices, as Daily caps and Usage estimates are.
- The engine's own reported cost is used only for a model with no price.
- Codex, Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI and OpenAI-compatible engines always report 0.
- See [5.2](5.2-stack.md), "Estimated cost".

### The Task attribute

- It is `office.task` everywhere.

### Metric attributes carry names

- They carry names (Role key, Stage name, Pipeline name, Project name), never ids.
- Ids appear only on spans and in the `office.engine.call` record.
- The one exception is `office.engine`, the engine's id: engines are few, and their names can change.

### The default range of Tokens and cost

- Tokens and cost keeps 7 d ([1 h is the default of System health](2.11.3-system-health.md#defaults)).
