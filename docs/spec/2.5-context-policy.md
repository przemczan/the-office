# 2.5 Context policy

- **Three moves:** **Resume** (same session, cache warm), **Fresh** (new session for the same person, briefed from the record), **Hand off** (another Role gets a brief; any Role change is a hand-off).
- **At a Stage boundary** the Stage's declared session wins. **Inside a Thread**, continuing resumes when the cache is warm (under 1 hour since the last call) or the context is small; when the cache is cold and the context large, The Office starts fresh with a brief. The size threshold is 30k tokens by default, set in Settings › Office limits. A Thread whose session can't resume starts fresh with a brief whatever the cache: its engine changed ([5.4](5.4-the-engine.md)), its Allow changes switch did ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md)), or the duties its person holds did ([2.2](2.2-people.md)).
- **The Brief** is assembled from the record with no model call: Task title, Description, Plan, Definition of Done, the Stage's brief, earlier Stage results, answered Decisions, the diff summary from git, the Attachment list, and the person's memory when they keep one. The Description is in the Planner's Brief and every Agent Stage's, worded alike, and "None: the title is all there is." when the Task has none. The Planner's Brief also says whether the Task is planned in auto mode, and so what marking it planned does ([2.1](2.1-work.md)). For a Task that is part of an Initiative, the Planner's Brief also has the Initiative, after the linked records: its name, state and outcome, its other Tasks in the order they were added, each with its Project, state, the Tasks it must wait for and its Plan, marked confirmed or not, with its files to change (a Plan over 2,000 characters left to read_task), and its Attachments by id; the Planner checks them for anything that bears on the Task and keeps its Plan consistent with the other Tasks' Plans. When the Initiative has a Pipeline, the Planner's Brief also says that the Task starts from and publishes into the Initiative's branch, and names the Initiative's Pipeline ([2.1](2.1-work.md#defaults)). The Brief also says which user asked: who created the Task and who started it, or, in a chat, who wrote the message it starts on ([2.10](2.10-users-and-signing-in.md)); answered Decisions say who answered them. Transcripts are never handed over.
- **Handover note:** every Agent Stage ends with a few lines (where I am, what is left, what to watch) inside its result.
- **Shown** on every Thread under the composer, in words with what and why ("Continues · cache warm, 41 min left"; "Starts fresh · cache cold, context large · Brief ~3k tokens"), with tokens per Thread and per Task. Above the message input, the session's context size against its model's window ("41k of 200k"), with a meter marking where The Office asks for a Handover note, amber from 70%, live while a turn runs; the words under the composer leave the size to it, so it shows once ([docs/design/session-context.html](../design/session-context.html)). The log's context lines keep their shorter words ("Continuing · cache warm · 41k context"; "Fresh · cache expired, 48k context · brief ~3k"). The line of a move that starts a session with a Brief expands to show the Brief as it was sent. On the Task page the To line marks the cache before the person's name: warm, with the minutes left of the cache's hour, or cold; a Thread with no session yet has no mark and says "Starts a session with a Brief ~3k tokens".
- **Overrides:** a per-Role default, per Stage, and per moment with **Auto**, **Continue** and **Start fresh**, a control under the composer that says what each would do and its estimated cost.
- **No automatic compaction:** near the context limit The Office asks for the handover note, ends the session and starts fresh with a brief. In a Stage's session The Office doesn't ask: the Stage's own Handover note ends it.

## Defaults

- **A person's session default decides only the cold case** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)): within an hour of a Thread's last call it continues, the cache being warm. Once cold, Context policy starts fresh above the threshold and continues below it, Always start fresh starts fresh, and Prefer to continue continues. "Start fresh" and "Continue" override it for the moment, and "Auto" goes back to it; a change of person is always a hand-off. Every layer names it the session default.
- **The Context policy's size threshold counts what the conversation added** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)) since the session's first turn: its context then (the engine's own prompt and tools, some 28–33k, the Brief and the first exchange) is what a fresh session takes too.
- **A chat Thread's Brief** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)), when it continues fresh: the Thread's title, the person's latest Handover note if one was written (The Office asks for one only near the context limit), and the last exchanges of The Office's log (the user's messages and the person's, not tool activity), newest kept, capped at about 3k tokens. The person's Role card and where the Projects are come with every session.
- **A person's Thread on a Task's Brief** ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md)): its first session's Brief is the record: the Task's title, Description, Plan, Definition of Done, Stage results, answered Decisions, the diff summary, and that person's Handover notes on this Task. A later fresh session (Start fresh, or a cold cache under Auto) is briefed by the Context policy only, as any chat Thread is; the record is not given again.
- **An Initiative's planning Thread's Brief** ([2.1](2.1-work.md), Plan the Initiative): the Initiative's record (its outcome, Tasks, their dependencies and Attachments), its Backlog Tasks to plan, each with the Tasks inside the Initiative it waits for, each Project it spans with its base branch and commands, the Pipeline library and whether auto mode is on. Where each Project's base branch is checked out, or, for an Initiative with a Pipeline, its Initiative's branch in the Projects it spans ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md)), comes with every session, as for a shaping Thread. A later fresh session is briefed from the record as it then stands, with the Tasks the Thread moved to Planning and hasn't marked planned yet, for it to finish, and a section **Waiting for an answer** listing the Decisions open in this Thread with their Tasks.
