# 2.5 Context policy

From [10](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/10-context-policy.md), with [02](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/02-agent-engines.md) and [03](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/03-factory-reference.md).

- **Three moves:** **Resume** (same session, cache warm), **Fresh** (new session for the same person, briefed from the record), **Hand off** (another Role gets a brief; any Role change is a hand-off).
- **At a Stage boundary** the Stage's declared session wins. **Inside a Thread**, continuing resumes when the cache is warm (under 1 hour since the last call) or the context is small; when the cache is cold and the context large, The Office starts fresh with a brief. The size threshold starts at 30k tokens and is tuned from telemetry. A Thread whose session can't resume starts fresh with a brief whatever the cache: its engine changed ([5.4](5.4-the-engine.md)), or its Allow changes switch did ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md); the user's answers of 2026-11-03).
- **The Brief** is assembled from the record with no model call: Task title, Description, Plan, Definition of Done, the Stage's brief, earlier Stage results, answered Decisions, the diff summary from git, the Attachment list, and the person's memory when they keep one. The Description is in the Planner's Brief and every Agent Stage's, worded alike, and "None: the title is all there is." when the Task has none (the user, 2026-11-05). The Planner's Brief also says whether the Task is planned in auto mode, and so what marking it planned does ([2.1](2.1-work.md)). For a Task that is part of an Initiative, the Planner's Brief also has the Initiative, after the linked records: its name, state and outcome, its other Tasks in the order they were added, each with its Project, state, the Tasks it must wait for and its Plan, marked confirmed or not, with its files to change (a Plan over 2,000 characters left to read_task), and its Attachments by id; the Planner checks them for anything that bears on the Task and keeps its Plan consistent with the other Tasks' Plans. Transcripts are never handed over.
- **Handover note:** every Agent Stage ends with a few lines (where I am, what is left, what to watch) inside its result.
- **Shown** on every Thread under the composer, in words with what and why ("Continues · cache warm, 41 min left · 41k context"; "Starts fresh · cache cold, 48k context · Brief ~3k tokens"), with tokens per Thread and per Task. The log's context lines keep their shorter words ("Continuing · cache warm · 41k context"; "Fresh · cache expired, 48k context · brief ~3k") (the user, 2026-11-05). The line of a move that starts a session with a Brief expands to show the Brief as it was sent. On the Task page the To line marks the cache before the person's name: warm, with the minutes left of the cache's hour, or cold; a Thread with no session yet has no mark and says "Starts a session with a Brief ~3k tokens" (the user, 2026-11-05).
- **Overrides:** a per-Role default, per Stage, and per moment with **Auto**, **Continue** and **Start fresh**, a control under the composer that says what each would do and its estimated cost (the user, 2026-11-05).
- **No automatic compaction:** near the context limit The Office asks for the handover note, ends the session and starts fresh with a brief.

## Defaults

Gaps no ticket settled, filled with the simplest choice consistent with the tickets and accepted by the user; each says when.

- **A person's session default decides only the cold case** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)): within an hour of a Thread's last call it continues, the cache being warm. Once cold, Context policy starts fresh above the threshold and continues below it, Always start fresh starts fresh, and Prefer to continue continues. "Start fresh" and "Continue" override it for the moment, and "Auto" goes back to it; a change of person is always a hand-off. Every layer names it the session default. (added while building tier 1, accepted 2026-10-25)
- **The Context policy's size threshold counts what the conversation added** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)) since the session's first turn: its context then (the engine's own prompt and tools, some 28–33k measured in task 09, the Brief and the first exchange) is what a fresh session takes too. 30k of that to start. (added while building tier 1, accepted 2026-10-25)
- **A chat Thread's Brief** ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)), when it continues fresh: the Thread's title, the person's latest Handover note if one was written (The Office asks for one only near the context limit), and the last exchanges of The Office's log (the user's messages and the person's, not tool activity), newest kept, capped at about 3k tokens. The person's Role card and where the Projects are come with every session. (added while building tier 1, accepted 2026-10-25)
- **A person's Thread on a Task's Brief** ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md); the user's answer of 2026-11-05): its first session's Brief is the record: the Task's title, Description, Plan, Definition of Done, Stage results, answered Decisions, the diff summary, and that person's Handover notes on this Task. A later fresh session (Start fresh, or a cold cache under Auto) is briefed by the Context policy only, as any chat Thread is; the record is not given again (the user, 2026-11-05).
