# 2.5 Context policy

Covers: the three moves, resume or fresh, the Brief, the Handover note, what a Thread shows, overrides, compaction, a Stage's context limits.

## The three moves

- **Resume**: same session, cache warm.
- **Fresh**: new session for the same person, briefed from the record.
- **Hand off**: another Role gets a brief; any Role change is a hand-off.

## Choosing a move

- At a Stage boundary the Stage's declared session wins.
- Inside a Thread, continuing resumes when the cache is warm (under 1 hour since the last call) or the context is small.
- When the cache is cold and the context large, The Office starts fresh with a brief.
- The size threshold is 30k tokens by default, set in Settings › Office limits.
- A Thread whose session can't resume starts fresh with a brief whatever the cache, when:
  - its engine changed, a change of engine type included; a changed model or effort alone resumes ([5.4.5](5.4.5-engines-and-cascade.md#a-session-whose-engine-changes));
  - its Allow changes switch did ([2.4.1](2.4.1-threads.md#allow-changes));
  - the duties its person holds did ([2.2](2.2-people.md)).
- The fresh session after a change of engine type, or on a Stand-in Engine of another type, works with the person's model and effort for the new type ([5.4.6](5.4.6-stand-in-engines-and-usage-windows.md#stand-ins-of-another-engine-type)).

## The Brief

- It is assembled from the record with no model call.
- Every session's Brief, on every engine, starts with one line naming the [knowledge base](6-permissions-and-safety.md#the-knowledge-base)'s index at its path in the install, and that it is read-only; an install without it leaves the line out.
- It holds:
  - the Task title, Description, Plan and Definition of Done;
  - the Stage's brief and earlier Stage results;
  - answered Decisions;
  - the diff summary from git;
  - the Attachment list;
  - the person's memory, when they keep one.
- Transcripts are never handed over.
- The Description is in the Planner's Brief and every Agent Stage's, worded alike, and reads "None: the title is all there is." when the Task has none.
- The Planner's Brief also says whether the Task is planned in auto mode, and so what marking it planned does ([2.1](2.1-work.md)).
- The Planner's Brief names the Task's Kind and the commit type it suggests (Bug `fix`, Feature `feat`, Chore `chore`), a Bug's Caused by and Found in version when set, and the Pipeline the Project gives the Kind, to propose unless the work needs another ([2.1.5](2.1.5-planning-and-start.md#the-kind-and-the-pipeline-while-planning)); an Initiative's planning Thread's Brief does the same per Task.
- Every Agent Stage's instructions say what to do with an issue found along the way: a small one (a typo, a missing check, a broken test beside the change) is fixed within the Task and named in the Handover note; one too big for the Task or outside its scope is proposed as a Bug Task with `propose_bug_task`, the Stage ended with needs_decision, and the work goes on once the answer comes, whichever it is, a Fix now answer fixing the issue within the Task first ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md#the-bug-task-decision)).
- `read_task` names a Task's Kind with its state and priority.
- The Brief says which user asked: who created the Task and who started it, or, in a chat, who wrote the message it starts on ([2.10](2.10-users-and-signing-in.md)); answered Decisions say who answered them.

### The Planner's Brief for an Initiative's Task

- For a Task that is part of an Initiative, the Planner's Brief also has the Initiative, after the linked records:
  - its name, state and outcome;
  - its other Tasks in the order they were added, each with its Project, state, the Tasks it must wait for and its Plan, marked confirmed or not, with its files to change (a Plan over 2,000 characters is left to read_task);
  - its Attachments by id.
- The Planner checks them for anything that bears on the Task and keeps its Plan consistent with the other Tasks' Plans.
- When the Initiative has a Pipeline, the Planner's Brief also says that the Task starts from and publishes into the Initiative's branch, and names the Initiative's Pipeline ([2.1](2.1-work.md#defaults)).

## Handover note

- Every Agent Stage ends with a few lines (where I am, what is left, what to watch) inside its result.
- An Agent Stage may also end a session early with `handing_over` and one ([A Stage's session has a Soft limit and a Hard limit](#a-stages-session-has-a-soft-limit-and-a-hard-limit)).

## What a Thread shows

- Under the composer, on every Thread, in words with what and why: "Continues · cache warm, 41 min left"; "Starts fresh · cache cold, context large · Brief ~3k tokens".
- Tokens are shown per Thread and per Task.
- Above the message input, the session's context size against its model's window ("41k of 200k"), with a meter marking where The Office asks for a Handover note, amber from 70%, live while a turn runs ([docs/design/session-context.html](../design/session-context.html)).
- The words under the composer leave the size to the meter, so it shows once.
- The log's context lines keep their shorter words: "Continuing · cache warm · 41k context"; "Fresh · cache expired, 48k context · brief ~3k".
- The line of a move that starts a session with a Brief expands to show the Brief as it was sent.
- On the Task page the To line marks the cache before the person's name: warm, with the minutes left of the cache's hour, or cold.
- A Thread with no session yet has no mark and says "Starts a session with a Brief ~3k tokens".

## Overrides

- A per-Role default, per Stage, and per moment with **Auto**, **Continue** and **Start fresh**.
- The per-moment control is under the composer and says what each would do and its estimated cost.

## No automatic compaction

- Near the context limit The Office asks for the handover note, ends the session and starts fresh with a brief.
- A chat Thread's session is replaced as above.
- An Agent Stage's session restarts at its own limits ([A Stage's session has a Soft limit and a Hard limit](#a-stages-session-has-a-soft-limit-and-a-hard-limit)).

## Defaults

### A Stage's session has a Soft limit and a Hard limit

- The Office's limits are a Soft limit of 100k tokens and a Hard limit of 180k, set in Settings › Office limits (soft at least 50k, hard at least the soft plus 20k, in steps of 5k).
- An Agent Stage's `contextLimits` field takes **Office default**, **Set for this Stage** (its own soft and hard) or **No limit** (the session never restarts on context size); a Script Stage and a Gate don't have it, and a Project Pipeline Change can set or clear it.
- While the Stage runs, the hard limit is capped at the chat hand-over share of the model's window (80%) and the soft limit at the hard; an engine that doesn't report context gets no limits.
- Past the Soft limit, once per session, The Office logs a line and sends the Agent the Prompt template `conversations.stage-soft-limit` at its next step.
  - If the Stage is nearly done, the Agent finishes it and ends as usual.
  - Otherwise it finishes the part it is on, leaves the worktree consistent with its checks green, and ends the turn with the result status `handing_over` and a Handover note.
- Past the Hard limit The Office stops the turn, logs a line and sends `conversations.stage-hard-limit`, which asks for the `handing_over` result alone.
  - On an engine that can't interrupt a turn, the session ends instead and the fresh session's Brief says no Handover note was written.
- The note names the files and line ranges the next session must read again, what is done, what is left and what to watch.
- `handing_over` is accepted only after The Office asked for it in that session; otherwise it gets the one correction, so there is no loop.
- On `handing_over` the Stage run goes on in a fresh session in the same Stage Thread, as [2.3.1](2.3.1-stages.md#a-stage-run-restarts-in-a-fresh-session-at-its-limits) says.

### A person's session default decides only the cold case

- Within an hour of a Thread's last call it continues, the cache being warm.
- Once cold, Context policy starts fresh above the threshold and continues below it, Always start fresh starts fresh, and Prefer to continue continues.
- "Start fresh" and "Continue" override it for the moment, and "Auto" goes back to it.
- A change of person is always a hand-off.
- Every layer names it the session default.

### The Context policy's size threshold counts what the conversation added

- It counts since the session's first turn: its context then (the engine's own prompt and tools, some 28–33k, the Brief and the first exchange) is what a fresh session takes too.

### A chat Thread's Brief

- It applies when the Thread continues fresh.
- It holds the Thread's title.
- It holds the person's latest Handover note if one was written (The Office asks for one only near the context limit).
- It holds the last exchanges of The Office's log (the user's messages and the person's, not tool activity), newest kept, capped at about 3k tokens.
- The person's Role card and where the Projects are come with every session.

### A person's Thread on a Task's Brief

- See [2.4.1](2.4.1-threads.md#a-persons-thread-on-a-task).
- Its first session's Brief is the record: the Task's title, Description, Plan, Definition of Done, Stage results, answered Decisions, the diff summary, and that person's Handover notes on this Task.
- A later fresh session (Start fresh, or a cold cache under Auto) is briefed by the Context policy only, as any chat Thread is; the record is not given again.

### An Initiative's planning Thread's Brief

- See [2.1](2.1-work.md), Plan the Initiative.
- It holds the Initiative's record: its outcome, Tasks, their dependencies and Attachments.
- It holds its Backlog Tasks to plan, each with the Tasks inside the Initiative it waits for.
- It holds each Project the Initiative spans with its base branch and commands.
- It holds the Pipeline library and whether auto mode is on.
- Where each Project's base branch is checked out comes with every session, as for a shaping Thread; for an Initiative with a Pipeline, its Initiative's branch in the Projects it spans does ([2.4.1](2.4.1-threads.md#what-a-thread-reads)).
- A later fresh session is briefed from the record as it then stands.
  - It lists the Tasks the Thread moved to Planning and hasn't marked planned yet, for it to finish.
  - It has a section **Waiting for an answer** listing the Decisions open in this Thread with their Tasks.
