# 2.4.3 Consultations

Covers: a Consultation, calls from chats, what both Threads show, Proposals in a Consultation, the callee's permissions, Ask me in a Consultation.

## Consultation

- A Consultation is a sub-thread between an asking person and a Consultant, labelled with both.
- It answers a Decision's form ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md)), approves a held call past a Hard switch, or takes a Request ([2.2](2.2-people.md)).
- On a Task the user can read it live or take it over, but not join it.
- The Decision's form stays locked until the user presses Take it over, which only unlocks it, so a stray click answers nothing; the answer takes it over ([Decision card](screens/decision-card.md)).
- On the Task page it shows in the Consultant's tab ([2.4.1](2.4.1-threads.md#a-persons-thread-on-a-task)).
- The Consultant answers in a fresh session with its memory and the brief of its home: the Task's, or their chat's.

## Calls from chats

- A person's chat (the Default person to talk to's included), their Thread on a Task, the Planning Thread and an Initiative's shaping and planning Threads can call a hired Consultant, as Agent Stages consult.
- A call from an Initiative's Thread is on no Task, as a chat's is, except a planning Thread's call naming a `taskId`, which is on that Task as its Decisions are.
- From a chat the call is a Decision on no Task, put to the callee, one at a time per Thread.
- The callee's Consultation opens in the callee's own chat and shows in its Thread list.
- The caller's turn ends and the answer comes as their next message, which they tell the user.
- A message the user writes meanwhile goes to the caller as usual.
- A chat has no Needs you: a Decision waiting on the user there shows inline in the chat, as the form, answered and sent in place, with no Needs you card and no Notification.
- The person in a chat may open one asking the user directly, one Decision open at a time per Thread whether a call or not.
- A call that falls back to the user becomes that inline form in the caller's chat. It falls back when it names nobody who can be called, is passed on to the user, or the Consultation couldn't start.
- A Request naming the caller's own Role is refused instead, never put to the user ([2.2](2.2-people.md#calls)).
- The user takes over a call only on a Task.

## Both Threads show it

- The caller's Thread shows the Decision card, naming the callee, then their answer; from a chat it links to the Consultation.
- The Consultation opens with what it was asked as The Office's line ("Angela asks you to: …", the form's questions in order).
- The Consultation logs the callee's answer as another ("Your answer to Angela: …").
- The callee answers the same form as the user would, Submit or Ask me again.
- An approval stays Allow once, Deny or Pass on ([2.2](2.2-people.md)).

## Proposals in a Consultation

- A Consultation on a Task shows its log, Proposal cards included, only while it waits on its Consultant; once answered, taken over, passed on or closed, its tab shows only its note ([2.4.1](2.4.1-threads.md#a-persons-thread-on-a-task)).
- As it closes, The Office declines every Proposal still open in it, as nobody can open its card to answer it any more; the card reads "Declined as its Consultation closed".
- The Office also does so at each start, for any left open before.
- A Consultation in a chat stays in the callee's Thread list, so its Proposals stay open there until the user answers them.

## The callee's own permissions

- The callee works with their own [Office tools](6-permissions-and-safety.md#a-persons-office-tools) and permissions, never the caller's, so a read-only Thread can't get a change made by calling someone ([6](6-permissions-and-safety.md#a-consultations-permissions)).
- What a Consultation may change, and its one named exception, are in [6](6-permissions-and-safety.md#a-consultations-permissions).
- **Ask me reads as Consult the user:** a call the callee's [Authority](6-permissions-and-safety.md#authority) holds at Ask me is held on one approval card, Allow once or Deny ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md#proposals-and-approvals)), and the Consultation waits on it.
- That card shows where a Consultation's question to the user shows: on a Task, in Needs you and on the Task; from a chat, inline in the caller's chat ([Calls from chats](#calls-from-chats)).
- The user isn't a person in the chain of calls, so the approval doesn't count against the two people asked ([2.2](2.2-people.md#escalation)).
- A call held at Consult _person_ goes to that person while the chain allows one more, and to the user once it doesn't.
- A Consultant who holds Onboards and repairs Projects sets a Project up when asked ([2.2](2.2-people.md#setting-a-project-up)).
