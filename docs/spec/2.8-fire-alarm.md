# 2.8 Fire alarm

From [15](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/15-fire-alarm.md) (tier 2).

- Office-wide only. **Sound the alarm** stops every Stage, waiting Task, Consultation and background work at the next safe point (hard stop after 60 s); running git operations finish; nothing new starts.
- Chats stay open, but their Agents lose tools at once, unless the user lifts that for one Thread; an Agent at work on a Stage finishes its current turn first (its safe point, or the hard stop).
- **All clear** first lists on Now what will resume, how (warm or fresh with a brief) and at what cost, with Hold and Drop per Task; then everything resumes through the Context policy.

## Defaults

Gaps no ticket settled, filled with the simplest choice consistent with the tickets and accepted by the user; each says when.

- **Acting during the alarm** (the user's answer of 2026-10-29): an answered Decision or a Gate action is recorded, but nothing walks until the all clear; Start joins the queue with the reason "The fire alarm is on.", released after the all clear. (added while building tier 2)
- **The alarm's details** (chosen while building tier 2, told to the user on 2026-10-29): Sound the alarm needs no confirmation; it outlives a restart; a Script Stage stops at once and runs again from its start; a lift for one Thread ends at the all clear; Hold leaves a Task paused as the user's own Pause; the cost is counted in tokens from the Context policy. (added while building tier 2)
- **Stopping and resuming during the alarm** (the user's answers of 2026-10-29): a Stage's Agent keeps its tools until its turn ends or the hard stop, while a chat's Agent loses them at once unless lifted for its Thread; a Resume while the alarm is on is recorded, the Task stays "Paused by the alarm" and resumes at the all clear; a pause by the alarm raises no "Task paused" Notification and no Outcome, as Now's alarm panel lists what the alarm paused. (added while building tier 2)
- **The alarm and an Initiative's Pause** (settled with the Plan of Task 4230f953, 2026-11-12): pausing an Initiative while the alarm is on takes over its Tasks the alarm paused as the Initiative's own, so the all clear doesn't resume them; resuming the Initiative while the alarm is on leaves its Tasks held by the alarm, and they resume at the all clear, as a Resume during the alarm does. A Task held by the Initiative's Pause isn't listed for the all clear ([2.1](2.1-work.md#defaults), "Pause the Initiative").
- **The alarm, an Initiative's Pause and the usage window** (Task 3de35a58, 2026-11-13): a Task paused by the usage window stays so while the alarm is sounded, and isn't listed for the all clear; when its reset comes during the alarm, the Task is held by the alarm instead and resumes at the all clear, as a Resume during the alarm does. Pause the Initiative takes such a Task over as the Initiative's own, as it takes over the alarm's, so the reset doesn't resume it; Resume the Initiative after the reset resumes it, and before the reset leaves it paused by the usage window until then.
