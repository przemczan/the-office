# 2.8 Fire alarm

Covers: sounding the alarm, its three ways, what stops and what goes on, making it stricter, chats and Actions during the alarm, the all clear, acting while the alarm is on.

## The alarm

- Office-wide only.
- **Sound the alarm** is a menu of three ways to stop; the alarm keeps the way it was sounded with.
- Under every way nothing new starts: Start and the queue start no Task.
- Running git operations finish.

## Safe pause ASAP

- Stops every Stage, waiting Task, Consultation and background work at the next safe point.
- Pauses every started Task by the alarm.
- The hard stop comes 60 s after it is sounded.

## Safe pause after the current Stages

- Each running Stage run goes on to its end, with any Decision it opens, Consultation it calls, and the answer to either.
- The walk starts no new Stage run: at the Stage boundary the Task becomes Paused by the alarm.
- A Gate counts as a Stage boundary.
- A run that goes on with a Stage whose last run asked the user a Decision is still that Stage's work, so it runs; the Task is paused once that Stage ends.
- No hard stop.

## Safe pause after the current Tasks

- Every started Task walks on to its end, or until a Gate, a Decision, a Pause or the usage window stops it, as usual.
- No queued or new Task starts.
- No hard stop.

## Making it stricter

- While a softer way is on, it can be sounded again in a stricter way: after the current Tasks → after the current Stages or ASAP; after the current Stages → ASAP.
- Sounding a stricter way changes the way; the alarm stays on since its first sounding.
- Made ASAP, it does what sounding ASAP does: pauses the started Tasks, and the hard stop comes 60 s from then.
- A softer way is refused: only the all clear eases the alarm.

## Chats during the alarm

- Chats stay open.
- Under the two softer ways, chats keep their tools and Consultations keep working.
- Once the alarm is ASAP, their Agents lose tools at once, unless the user lifts that for one Thread.
- An Agent at work on a Stage finishes its current turn first (its safe point, or the hard stop).

## Actions during the alarm

- Under every way, a new Action start ([2.15](2.15-actions.md)) is refused, "The fire alarm is on."; it is never queued.
- Under ASAP, every running Project command run stops at once: Stopped, "Stopped by the fire alarm." ([2.15](2.15-actions.md#when-a-run-stops)).
- Under the two softer ways, a running Project command run goes on to its end.
- Test instances, those an Action launched among them, and CI runs, those an Action started among them, go on: the alarm stops none of them.
- The all clear lets Actions start again; it restarts no run the alarm stopped.

## All clear

- **All clear** first lists on Now what will resume, how (warm, or fresh with a brief) and at what cost.
- Each listed Task has Hold and Drop.
- Then everything resumes through the [Context policy](2.5-context-policy.md), and the queue is released.
- A Task still running under a softer way isn't listed; it walks on as it was.

## Defaults

### Acting during the alarm

- Under ASAP, an answered Decision or a Gate action is recorded, but nothing walks until the all clear.
- Under a softer way, an answered Decision or a Gate action walks on, up to the next Stage boundary under after the current Stages.
- Start joins the queue with the reason "The fire alarm is on.", released after the all clear.

### The alarm's details

- Sound the alarm needs no confirmation.
- The alarm outlives a restart, with its way.
- At a restart under after the current Stages, a run left running is held as under ASAP (its Task paused by the alarm), as the restart cut it; under after the current Tasks the walk goes on.
- A Script Stage stops at once under ASAP and runs again from its start.
- A lift for one Thread ends at the all clear.
- Hold leaves a Task paused as the user's own Pause.
- The cost is counted in tokens from the Context policy.

### Stopping and resuming during the alarm

- A Stage's Agent keeps its tools until its turn ends or the hard stop.
- A chat's Agent loses its tools at once under ASAP, unless lifted for its Thread.
- A Resume of a Task paused by the alarm is recorded; the Task stays "Paused by the alarm" and resumes at the all clear.
- Under ASAP every Resume is held so; under a softer way a Resume of a Task the user paused goes on as when the alarm is off.
- A pause by the alarm raises no "Task paused" Notification and no Outcome; Now's alarm panel lists what the alarm paused, and the Tasks still running under a softer way.

### The alarm and an Initiative's Pause

- Pausing an Initiative while the alarm is on takes over its Tasks the alarm paused as the Initiative's own, so the all clear doesn't resume them.
- Resuming the Initiative while the alarm is ASAP leaves its Tasks held by the alarm; they resume at the all clear, as a Resume during the alarm does.
- A Task held by the Initiative's Pause isn't listed for the all clear ([2.1](2.1.4-initiatives.md#pause-the-initiative), "Pause the Initiative").

### The alarm, an Initiative's Pause and the usage window

- A Task paused by the usage window stays so while the alarm is sounded, and isn't listed for the all clear.
- When its reset comes while the alarm is ASAP, the Task is held by the alarm instead and resumes at the all clear, as a Resume during the alarm does; under a softer way it resumes at the reset.
- Pause the Initiative takes such a Task over as the Initiative's own, as it takes over the alarm's, so the reset doesn't resume it.
- Resume the Initiative after the reset resumes it; before the reset, it stays paused by the usage window until then.
