# 2.7 Notifications

Covers: what a Notification is, kinds, channels, content, routing, quiet hours, batching, what Now and the tab count, Decisions in chats.

## A Notification

- It is a pointer to something that waits or happened.
- It opens that thing and is cleared on every channel by handling it.
- There is no inbox: Needs you and Since you left serve as one.

## Kinds

- Kinds are a tree of hierarchical keys (`needs-you.decision`, `office.window.used-up`).
- Routing is stored at any level and inherited from the nearest one, so splitting a kind keeps every setting.
- **Needs you**: Decision waiting, Gate waiting, a message to the user.
- **Outcomes**: Task done, failed or paused; Initiative done; a test instance Running, Built or Failed.
- **Office**: a usage window nearly used, used up or reset; the service or an engine down; the alarm lifted with work waiting; The Office updated, or an Office update failed.
- A message to the user and the alarm lifted raise nothing: their rows are in the grid only.
- Never notified: Stage progress, Tasks starting, Consultations the user is not in, Agents talking to each other, approvals made by colleagues.

## Channels

- Channels are adapters behind one port.
- In-app: toast, Needs you counted in the tab title and favicon, status strip.
- Desktop.

## Content

- The person's avatar and name, one first-person line, the Task and Project.
- Office kinds come from the holder of Default person to talk to, or from the Caretaker while nobody holds it, and open that person's chat.
- "Initiative done" comes from the holder of Shapes Initiatives.
- A test instance's Outcome is in the UI's own voice, as the user launched it themselves.
- There are no action buttons.

## Routing

- Needs you goes in-app and to the desktop.
- Outcomes go in-app and to the desktop.
- Office kinds go in-app, and "service or engine down" also to the desktop.
- A desktop notification appears only when the tab isn't focused; otherwise the in-app toast shows.
- Needs you is never batched and there are no repeat reminders.
- Settings › Notifications holds the kinds × channels grid, quiet hours and the batching of Outcomes.
- The Project filter never silences.

## Defaults

### Office kinds

- They show in the status strip and as in-app toasts.
- They come from the holder of Default person to talk to (or the Caretaker), with a short line for the strip's slot in the UI's own voice (sentence case, no "I").
- "Service or engine down" also goes to the desktop.
- They never appear as "System".
- The web raises "service down" itself, as the service can't while the web has lost it.

### A failed Task

- Work has no Failed state.
- A Task failed when a Stage failed with no retries left, or its Pipeline ended with the Definition of Done not met.
- It shows as failed in Since you left while The Office's Decision about it waits in Needs you.
- Dropping a Task is the user's own act, not an outcome, and closes its open Decisions, The Office's among them ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md)).

### Since you left begins after five idle minutes

- The web reports the user active while the tab is visible and used; five minutes without activity means they left.
- Since you left lists each Task's latest outcome from that moment on, and keeps them for the rest of the visit.

### Window nearly used at 90%

- "5-hour window nearly used" is raised as the window crosses 90%, and "reset" as its use drops.
- Opening Now handles both.

### A usage window used up

- It is the Office kind `office.window.used-up`, in the `office.window` row of the grid.
- It is raised once per engine and window when the engine refuses a turn for it: "Claude Code's 5-hour window is used up until 14:00. Tasks on it are paused and wait."
- It is handled at the window's reset, or by opening Now.
- A Task the usage window paused raises its "Task paused" Outcome, worded "Paused: Claude Code's 5-hour window is used up. Resumes at 14:00.", handled by the resume, by itself or by hand.

### Stages waiting for a Stand-in Engine

- When a Stage is held because its session's context is over the size set in Settings › Engines, The Office sends one Notification of kind `needs-you.decision`, spoken by the holder of Default person to talk to: "2 paused Stages wait for your choice: switch to Claude Code 2, or wait for the reset at 14:00."
- It is for the one card in Needs you ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md#defaults), "The Decision on Stages waiting for a Stand-in Engine"), on Now and narrowed on the Task's page.
- A Stage that pauses this way while the card shows joins it, with no second Notification.
- It clears on its own when no Stage waits.
- Such a Task raises no separate "Task paused" Outcome.
- The Engine's used-up-window Notification is raised as well.

### Opening what a Notification points to handles it

- A Decision is handled by answering it.
- A Gate is handled by acting at it.
- A Task's outcome is handled by opening the Task; a pause also by resuming, a failure also by answering the Decision about it.
- The window's Office kinds are handled by opening Now.
- "Engine down" is handled by the engine answering again.
- An Office update's is handled by opening Settings › Updates.
- A test instance's is handled by opening the page it opens, or by the instance moving to another state.

### Quiet hours hold the desktop

- In-app is unchanged inside the window, and "service or engine down" passes through it.
- The one summary at its end goes to the channels the held Notifications would have reached.

### Batching Outcomes

- The first Outcome goes at once.
- Later ones within the window replace it with one Notification in the UI's voice ("3 Tasks done") that opens Since you left and is cleared once every outcome in it is handled.
- A test instance's Outcome is never batched, as Since you left lists no test instances; quiet hours' summary counts it among the Outcomes.

### The grid's rows

- Each row is a level of the tree.
- The Office kinds have a level between the group and the kind: `office.window` (nearly used, used up, reset), `office.down` (service, engine) and `office.update` (done, failed), beside `office.alarm-lifted`.
- Outcomes have one such level, `outcomes.test-instance` (Running, Built, Failed), one row: "A test instance Running, Built or Failed".

### Test instance Outcomes

- A test instance ([2.12](2.12-test-instances.md)) becoming Running, Built or Failed raises `outcomes.test-instance.running`, `.built` or `.failed`.
- They have no routing of their own until the user sets their row, so they go where Outcomes go: in-app and to the desktop.
- The line names the target, then its Initiative, or its branch when it is linked to none: "Web app in Billing is Running.", "Android app in Billing is Built: app.apk.", "Web app on feature/search failed: Exited with code 1."
- A Build to install instance failed by the user's Stop, or by its Initiative ending, raises nothing.
- It opens the instance's Initiative page, or the Project's Test targets for one linked to none, with the Test instances fold open and its row in view.
- Its next state clears it, so only the latest is open.

### Initiatives being shaped on Now

- While an Initiative is in Shaping, Now's Running panel shows a Shaping section between the Queue and Since you left, oldest first.
- One card per Initiative shows its name, its Projects so far, who holds Shapes Initiatives (or "Nobody shapes Initiatives yet"), its Tasks so far and how long it has been shaped.
- The card opens the Initiative's page.
- The Project filter narrows it, with its "in other Projects" note; one with no Tasks yet always shows.
- Now is announced again as an Initiative is made, confirmed or dropped, as its Tasks come and go or move Project, and as who holds Shapes Initiatives changes.

### Today's figures

- "Today" runs from local midnight on the service.
- Tokens today count input, output and cache writes, leaving out cache reads, as every token count in The Office does (a Thread's, a Stage's, a Usage estimate's, Now's and the strip's).
- An info icon beside a count gives all four kinds and what each costs.

### The tab counts Needs you and blinks for a new item

- The tab's title reads "(2) The Office" and the favicon shows 2 for every Decision and Gate waiting, across all Projects.
- Outcomes and Office kinds still toast, reach the desktop and show in the strip, but don't count in the tab.
- Waiting for you ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md#waiting-for-you)) never counts in the tab title or the favicon, and raises no Notification.
- When a new item arrives while the tab is hidden, the title alternates every second with "2 need you" ("1 needs you") and the favicon with the plain icon, until the tab shows again or nothing waits.
- Items there when the page loads, or arriving while the tab shows, don't blink.
- With reduced motion preferred, nothing blinks.

### A Decision in a chat isn't in Needs you

- A Decision waiting on the user in a person's chat shows inline in the chat, as its form, and nowhere else: no Needs you item, no Notification and no count in the tab ([2.4.2](2.4.2-decisions.md)).
- A Decision opened in a Thread on a Task, a Stage Thread, a Consultation or an Initiative's shaping or planning Thread is a Needs you item.
- It is one item per form however many questions it has, cleared once it is submitted, asked again or closed.
