# 2.7 Notifications

From [20](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/20-notifications.md) (amended by [13](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/13-first-slice.md)).

- **A Notification is a pointer** to something that waits or happened; it opens that thing and is cleared on every channel by handling it. No inbox: Needs you and Since you left serve as one.
- **Kinds are a tree of hierarchical keys** (`needs-you.decision`, later `needs-you.decision.limit-reached`); routing overrides are stored at any level and inherited from the nearest one, so splitting a kind keeps every setting. Groups today: **Needs you** (Decision waiting, Gate waiting, a message to the user), **Outcomes** (Task done, failed or paused; Initiative done), **Office** (5-hour window nearly used, used up or reset; the service or an engine down; the alarm lifted with work waiting). Never notified: Stage progress, Tasks starting, Consultations the user is not in, Agents talking to each other, approvals made by colleagues.
- **Channels** are adapters behind one port: in-app (toast, unread count in tab title and favicon, status strip) and desktop in tier 1; phone push (Web Push over HTTPS on the LAN with a local certificate authority, installed app) in tier 2; email and chat apps in tier 3.
- **Content:** the person's avatar and name, one first-person line, the Task and Project. Office kinds come from the Office Manager and open the Office Manager's chat. No action buttons.
- **Routing defaults** apply from tier 1, without the phone column: Needs you goes in-app and to the desktop; Outcomes go in-app and to the desktop; Office kinds go in-app, and "service or engine down" also to the desktop. A desktop notification appears only when the tab isn't focused; otherwise the in-app toast shows. Needs you is never batched and there are no repeat reminders.
- **The kinds × channels grid, the at-desk hold, quiet hours and batching of Outcomes** are tier 2, as in [20](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/20-notifications.md). The Project filter never silences.

## Defaults

Gaps no ticket settled, filled with the simplest choice consistent with the tickets and accepted by the user; each says when.

- **Office kinds in tier 1 have no person:** until the Office Manager is hired (tier 2), they show in the status strip and as in-app toasts in the UI's own voice (sentence case, no "I"), and "service or engine down" also goes to the desktop. They never appear as a message from a person, and never as "System". (accepted 2026-10-24)
- **No message from a person in tier 1** (the user's answer of 2026-10-27): nothing in tier 1 lets a person write to the user unprompted, so Needs you holds Decisions and Gates; the kind `needs-you.message` exists, and messages join when the Office Manager or the CTO can send them (tier 2). (added while building tier 1)
- **A failed Task** (the user's answer of 2026-10-27): Work has no Failed state; a Task failed when a Stage failed with no retries left, or its Pipeline ended with the Definition of Done not met. It shows as failed in Since you left while The Office's Decision about it waits in Needs you. Dropping a Task is the user's own act, not an outcome. (added while building tier 1)
- **Since you left begins after five idle minutes** (the user's answer of 2026-10-27): the web reports the user active while the tab is visible and used; five minutes without activity means they left. Since you left lists each Task's latest outcome from that moment on, and keeps them for the rest of the visit. (added while building tier 1)
- **Window nearly used at 90%** (chosen while building tier 1, told to the user on 2026-10-27): "5-hour window nearly used" is raised as the window crosses 90%, and "reset" as its use drops; opening Now handles both. (added while building tier 1)
- **A usage window used up** (Task 3de35a58, 2026-11-13): the Office kind `office.window.used-up`, in the `office.window` row of the grid, raised once per engine and window when the engine refuses a turn for it: "Claude Code's 5-hour window is used up until 14:00. Tasks on it are paused and wait." It is handled at the window's reset, or by opening Now. A Task the usage window paused raises its "Task paused" Outcome, worded "Paused: Claude Code's 5-hour window is used up. Resumes at 14:00.", handled by the resume, by itself or by hand.
- **Opening what a Notification points to handles it** (the user's answer of 2026-10-27): a Decision is handled by answering it, a Gate by acting at it, a Task's outcome by opening the Task (a pause also by resuming, a failure also by answering the Decision about it), the window's Office kinds by opening Now, and "engine down" by the engine answering again.
- **The phone in task 27, Web Push in task 28** (the user's answer of 2026-10-29): the phone is a channel in the routing and the grid (its column "Not set up yet") with ticket 20's defaults, and the at-desk hold and the away delay apply to it, sent through a port whose only adapter records until task 28 brings Web Push. The away delay counts from when the hold ends. (added while building tier 2)
- **Quiet hours hold the desktop and the phone** (the user's answer of 2026-10-29): in-app is unchanged inside the window; the one summary at its end goes to the channels the held Notifications would have reached. (added while building tier 2)
- **Batching Outcomes** (the user's answer of 2026-10-29): the first Outcome goes at once; later ones within the window replace it with one Notification in the UI's voice ("3 Tasks done") that opens Since you left and is cleared once every outcome in it is handled. (added while building tier 2)
- **The grid's rows** (chosen while building tier 2): the Office kinds gain a level, `office.window` (nearly used, used up, reset) and `office.down` (service, engine), and `office.alarm-lifted` joins them, so each row of the grid is a level of the tree. "Alarm lifted with work waiting" (the user's answer of 2026-10-29) stays in the grid and is raised from task 28, on the other devices, when the alarm is lifted on one. (added while building tier 2)
- **Today's figures** (accepted 2026-10-27): "today" runs from local midnight on the service; tokens today count input, output and cache writes, leaving out cache reads, as every token count in The Office does (a Thread's, a Stage's, a Usage estimate's, Now's and the strip's); an info icon beside a count gives all four kinds and what each costs (amended 2026-10-30, the user's answer); Tasks touched are the Tasks with a Stage run today. (added while building tier 1)
