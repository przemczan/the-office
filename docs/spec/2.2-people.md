# 2.2 People

From [04](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/04-agent-identity.md), [05](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/05-roles.md), [06](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/06-office-manager.md), [21](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/21-role-capabilities.md), [23](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/23-skills.md), [25](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/25-agent-permissions.md).

- **Role:** a job the user hires for, filled by one named person ("Ada · Developer"). Every person is Office-wide. There are no kinds of person, but one exception: the built-in **Caretaker**.
- **Agent:** a person at work on one thing (a Task or a conversation). A person can be at work on several things at once. A person's Agent on a Task stays the same person for each Stage on it naming that Role, or naming a duty they hold when the Stage starts (amended 2026-11-13).
- **Role card:** name and title; its responsibilities in one paragraph; the instructions; effort, and from tier 3 the engine and model as a **Role override** or none ("Office default"), the card showing which engine and model apply and where they come from ([5.4](5.4-the-engine.md#defaults), the engine cascade); **capabilities** (Shared memory, Chat, Consultant, Run environment), each switched freely; **Run environment** gives the person's Agent Stages the Office tool that writes the Project's run environment definition, `write_run_environment_definition` ([5.5](5.5-git-and-isolation.md), [6](6-permissions-and-safety.md)); the permission profile ([6](6-permissions-and-safety.md)); authority rules; **Hard switches**; **Task limit** (1–6, or none: the person only talks and no Pipeline may name the Role); **Skills**.
- **Duty:** a built-in job The Office gives to whichever hired Role holds it, set in Settings › Duties; Defaults below: "Duties", "Who holds a duty". (added 2026-11-13)
- **Avatar:** every person, the Caretaker included, can have their own picture, uploaded on their Overview tab: a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG of at most 2 MB, the kind read from its contents, shown as uploaded and cropped to the centre of a circle, in place of their initials wherever they are drawn, the initials and tint showing while there is none or it can't be loaded. It sits beside the Role card, not on it: a change adds no card version and no Proposal, and Agents never see it. Someone let go keeps showing theirs, unchanged until hired back. "You" and The Office have none.
- **Authority** in three tiers written as plain rules in the instructions: decide alone, consult *person* first, ask the user. **Hard switches** (run installs, publish outside the Pipeline, change Roles) are each Allowed, Consult *person*, Ask me or Never ([6](6-permissions-and-safety.md)).
- **Escalation:** a Consultant asked to approve answers Allow once, Deny or Pass on. **A missing person falls back to the user:** whenever an Agent would consult someone not hired, the user is asked; from a chat, which has no Needs you, the caller asks the user in their reply ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md)).
- **Calls** (Task c288eb09; the user, 2026-11-12: "a person should be able to call another person who can do what they can't (e.g. 'ask Devops to set that up')", "building on the existing consult mechanism so there is one mechanism, not two"): a call is a consult. Besides a question and an approval it may be a **Request**, asking the callee to do something, which they answer in their own words with what they did, such as a Proposal they drafted, or why they can't. The callee is a hired person with Consultant on who isn't the caller; the holder of Default person to talk to, in their chat, still redirects with a Link card rather than relaying, and calls someone only when the user asks them to (amended 2026-11-13; amended 2026-11-14, Task "Linking Tasks to Initiatives and to each other"). A person calls only when the user asks them to, or their authority says to consult that person first.
- **One chain of calls, two people at most** (the user, 2026-11-12: "limit the depth of calls (about 2) and forbid calling someone already in the chain"): a Consultation may call someone itself, and the chain is the first asker, every asker up its Consultations and everyone a Decision down it was put to or passed on to. At most two people are asked down one chain, nested calls and passes-on counted together (e.g. Planner → Devops → CTO, or Devops → CTO → the user): at the last step a Consultation has no way to call, and an approval passed on goes to the user. Calling or passing on to someone already in the chain is refused, naming them.
- **Presets** (hiring starts from a template; the template sets the switches):

  | | Memory | Chat | Consultant | Run environment | Task limit | Tier | Holds by default |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Planner | on (off until tier 2) | on | on | off | 2 | 1 | Plans |
  | Developer | off | off | off | on | 3 | 1 | Implements |
  | Reviewer | off | off | off | off | 3 | 1 | Reviews |
  | CTO | on | on | on | off | 2 | 2 | Shapes Initiatives, Asked first when a Stage runs out of tries |
  | Devops | on | on | on | on | 2 | 2 | Onboards and repairs Projects, Default Consultant for MCP tool hard switches |
  | Office Manager | on | on | off | off | none | 2 | Default person to talk to |

  The presets do their built-in jobs as these duties' holders by default ("Duties" below; amended 2026-11-13): the CTO shapes Initiatives and also owns architecture; Devops onboards and repairs Projects and owns the Pipeline library, Project Pipelines among the Pipelines it proposes, and Project commands by Proposal; the Planner co-writes Plans; the Reviewer is an optional independent review.
- **Office Manager** (tier 2): the preset that holds Default person to talk to by default (amended 2026-11-13). Drafts Roles and Office settings as **Proposals** the user confirms with one click; redirects with a link card and an editable handover note, never relays; captures Ideas; sees all of The Office read-only; never works a Stage, as its Task limit is none. Never alone: Roles, settings, starting, stopping or moving work, publishing, speaking for others. The chat tools tied to a preset's key follow the matching duty, so whoever holds it has them in their chat (amended 2026-11-13; details settled with the Plan of Task 70a243f5): Proposals of Roles and Office settings, Link cards, Office status, capturing Ideas and `create_initiative` go with Default person to talk to; the CTO's Initiative tools (`create_initiative` included) with Shapes Initiatives; Devops' Proposal tools (Pipelines, Project commands, Stage templates, the run environment) with Onboards and repairs Projects; the planning tools with Plans.
- **Caretaker:** the built-in person who looks after the workstation and The Office, present on a fresh database under the Role key `caretaker`. It never holds a duty, and is the default person to talk to while nobody holds that duty. At any time it may draft hires (Role card Proposals from presets) and duty assignments (Proposals of the Office setting), which the user confirms; other Office settings stay with the holder of Default person to talk to. On an Office with nobody hired it offers to set the Office up ("Setting up an Office with nobody hired" below). (amended 2026-11-13) Never hired and never let go; not offered as a preset, and nothing else can be hired into its key. Its card: built in, Chat and Shared memory on, not a Consultant, Run environment off, Task limit none, so no Pipeline can name it. Its name, title, responsibilities, instructions, effort, Role override, session default, Skills and MCP servers (with their tool settings) stay editable; its capabilities, permission profile, Hard switches and Task limit are fixed, and a change to them is refused. People pins it first with a "Built in" mark and no Let go action. Its chat only reads until the user turns on **Allow changes** in a Thread ([2.4](2.4-conversations.md), [6](6-permissions-and-safety.md)). (the user's answers of 2026-11-03)
- **Changing a Role** takes effect at the next session start. Before drafting a change to a Role, the Office Manager reads its whole card, instructions included, with `read_role`, since a change gives each field whole; so does an Agent before rewording a Role with `change_role`. **Letting a Role go** (tier 2) pauses its running Tasks behind a Decision and flags Pipelines that name it. It also leaves the duties it held unheld, so each falls back and the Pipelines naming those duties are flagged; hiring the same key back restores them, and renaming a Role changes none of its duties (amended 2026-11-13). **Switching a capability off** keeps its memory or threads, unused and readable; deletion only by explicit, confirmed actions on the Role card (tier 2).
- **Office instructions** (tier 3): one text the user writes in Settings › Office instructions, for general guidelines; every person's session gets it with their Role's instructions, on every engine. They are the general default: where they disagree, a Role's instructions and a Project's `CLAUDE.md` win, and the session is told so; the Office rules stand above all three. Left empty, they add nothing. As with a Role, a change takes effect at the next session start, and a resumed session keeps the version it started with. The Office Manager may draft a change as a Proposal of an Office setting, reading the current text first with `read_office_instructions`, since its session may hold an older version; a text changed since the draft is refused when applied. (the user's answers of 2026-11-06)
- **Shared memory** (tier 2): one append-only memory per person, read at session start, written as small notes. Its form is an open question ([8](8-open-questions-and-risks.md)).
- **Limits:** each person's Task limit, each Project's Project limit on its started Tasks ([2.1](2.1-work.md), "Project"), plus one Office-wide limit on running Tasks to protect quota, and from tier 3 each engine's Window caps and Daily caps ([2.1](2.1-work.md), "Usage estimates"). Talking never counts and is never blocked. A Task Start can't run for a blocker, a limit or a cap waits in the queue, counting against neither until it starts ([2.1](2.1-work.md), "A queue for Start").
- **What each person is doing now** (the Live view, Task "Service: what each person is doing now, for the Live view", 2026-11-15): one snapshot lists everyone filling a Role now, the Caretaker included; anyone let go is left out, so a person joining or leaving the snapshot is a hire, a hire back or a let-go. For each person it lists every activity:
  - **Working:** one Agent for each Thread they are answering in that waits on no Decision: a Stage Thread, a Consultation (with the Decision it answers and who asked it) or any other Thread. A Stage Thread waiting on its Decision isn't working; an Initiative's planning Thread is, while its Decisions wait, as the planner works on.
  - **Consulting:** each open Decision in their Threads put to a Consultant, naming the Consultant it waits on now (the one it was passed on to, if any), the question and when it was asked.
  - **Waiting on you:** each open Decision in their Threads waiting on the user, with the question and when it was asked; The Office's own Decision when a Stage runs out of tries, a budget or the Definition of Done shows on the person whose Stage Thread it was opened in, or as consulting while put to someone first.
  - **Idle:** none of these.
  Gates waiting on the user belong to no person and aren't shown.

## Defaults

Gaps no ticket settled, filled with the simplest choice consistent with the tickets and accepted by the user; each says when.

- **A Task waiting on the user or paused still counts** against its person's Task limit and the Office-wide limit, since it keeps its worktree and session. (accepted 2026-10-24)
- **The Office-wide limit on running Tasks** defaults to 3; the status strip's "desks in use" is the running Tasks against this limit. (accepted 2026-10-24)
- **Preset Task limits** are single numbers within [21](../../.scratch/the-office/issues/21-role-capabilities.md)'s ranges: Planner 2, Developer 3, Reviewer 3, CTO 2, Devops 2. (accepted 2026-10-24)
- **Duties** (the user's answers of 2026-11-13, the Initiative b62c4d5e; details settled with the Plan of Task 70a243f5): a **duty** is a built-in job The Office gives to whichever hired Role holds it, set in Settings › Duties. A Role **holds** a duty and is its **holder**. Each duty has a key, is held by today's Role by default, and falls back while nobody holds it:

  | Duty | Key | Held by default by | While nobody holds it |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Default person to talk to | `default-person-to-talk-to` | `office-manager` | the Caretaker |
  | Shapes Initiatives | `shapes-initiatives` | `cto` | the user, as "Shaping without a CTO" in [2.1](2.1-work.md#defaults) |
  | Asked first when a Stage runs out of tries | `asked-first-when-out-of-tries` | `cto` | the user |
  | Onboards and repairs Projects (owns the Pipeline library, Project commands and Skill installs) | `onboards-projects` | `devops` | the user |
  | Default Consultant for MCP tool hard switches | `mcp-tool-consultant` | `devops` | the user |
  | Plans | `plans` | `planner` | the user plans alone |
  | Implements | `implements` | `developer` | the Pipelines whose Stages name it are flagged and Start is refused, as for a Role nobody fills ([2.3](2.3-pipelines.md#defaults), "A Stage names a Role or a duty") |
  | Reviews | `reviews` | `reviewer` | as Implements |

  **Reading the spec:** where the spec names the Planner, the CTO, Devops or the Office Manager for one of these jobs, it means the person holding that duty. Passages are rewritten only where behaviour depends on a key being hired, a Stage names a Role, or a button, tab or tool names one.
- **Who holds a duty** (the user's answers of 2026-11-13, the Initiative b62c4d5e; details settled with the Plan of Task 70a243f5):
  - Each duty has exactly one holder, a hired Role, or none; one Role may hold several.
  - It is stored as an Office setting in The Office's records. A duty never set is held by its default Role key; it can be set to another hired Role or to none. Setting it to a Role not hired, or to the Caretaker, is refused.
  - A holder that isn't hired (let go, or never hired) counts as nobody, so letting a Role go leaves its duties unheld and hiring the same key back restores them. Duties name Role keys, so renaming a Role's title changes nothing.
  - A holder with Consultant off is passed over for Asked first when a Stage runs out of tries and Default Consultant for MCP tool hard switches, and the user is asked; Settings › Duties says so beside it.
  - A change reaches only what starts after it: a started Stage, an open Thread and a Consultation already put keep their person.
  - The Office Manager, that is the holder of Default person to talk to, drafts a duty change as a Proposal of an Office setting: it keeps the value as it was and as proposed, applies all or nothing, and is refused once the value changed since.
  - A Hard switch or an MCP tool may consult any hired Role, a preset's or one hired from scratch, not only the preset keys, so a Role hired from scratch can hold Default Consultant for MCP tool hard switches.
- **Duties on screen** (the user's answers of 2026-11-13, the Initiative b62c4d5e; details settled with the Plan of Task 70a243f5): Settings › Duties lists each duty with its holder, picked from the hired Roles or none, and its fallback while none. The Role card and People show the duties each person holds. Buttons and tabs that name a Role because of its duty ("Ask Devops", the CTO's tab on an Initiative, the default chat person) name the holder, as in "Ask Dwight"; while the duty is unheld they show the fallback and point to Settings › Duties. Details in [4](4-ux-direction.md).
- **Setting up an Office with nobody hired** (the user's answers of 2026-11-13, the Initiative b62c4d5e; details settled with the Plan of Task 70a243f5): the Caretaker is present on a fresh database. People's empty state and the preset list show which preset to hire for which duties and capabilities. The Caretaker's chat offers to set the Office up and drafts the hires and the duty assignments as Proposals the user confirms.
- **A person's headline state on the Live view** (Task "Service: what each person is doing now, for the Live view", 2026-11-15): with several activities at once, all are listed and the headline is waiting on you first, then consulting, then working, then idle. An idle person is idle since the later of their last engine call's end and their hiring.
