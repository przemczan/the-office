# 2.4.2 Decisions

Covers: the Decision form, Submit, Ask me again and Dismiss, the Bug Task Decision, where a Decision shows, Waiting for you, closing a Decision, answering for the user, waiting without blocking.

## The Decision form

- A Decision is a form the Agent opens in the Thread it works in.
- It has one or more **questions** in the order the Agent asks them, its context, and any of the Task's or the Initiative's Attachments it is about.
- A question is a **choice**: its options, with the Agent's recommended one preselected and the reason it gives for it.
- A question is otherwise a **choice of several**: its options as checkboxes, the user picking every one that applies, one or more, with the Agent's recommended ones checked at first and the reason it gives for them.
  - An Agent asks one by setting `several` on the question, with `recommended` a list of option numbers.
  - The service refuses one recommending no option, an option twice, or one it hasn't.
  - It is kept as a choice, its first recommended option the choice's, and its answer as the options' names in words, so the release before reads it as a choice answered in words.
- A question is otherwise an **open question**, answered in words.
- Every question takes an optional **comment**, and the form takes one **general comment**.
- A choice is answered by picking an option, a choice of several by picking one or more, each once; picking none, either is answered by its words alone, which are then the answer in words.
- The user may ask follow-ups in the same Thread before sending it.
- A Decision recorded before the form reads as a form of one question: a choice with its options, or an open question when it had none.

## Sending a Decision

- **Submit** sends every question's answer with its comment, and the general comment.
  - It needs an answer to every question.
  - The Decision is answered.
- **Ask me again** sends only the general comment, no answers, saying what to explain or change.
  - The Decision closes as **asked again**, leaves Needs you and its Notification clears.
  - The Agent explains and asks again in a new form.
  - The Office's own Decisions (a Stage out of tries, a budget, the Definition of Done, a restart loop, a moved branch) have no Ask me again: nobody is there to ask again.
- **Dismiss** closes the Decision unanswered, with an optional general comment.
  - The Decision closes as **dismissed**, leaves Needs you and its Notification clears.
  - It starts no turn: the asker learns of it with the user's next message in that Thread, which carries ahead of it the note `You dismissed the Decision “<summary>”: <comment>. Its questions no longer apply; don't ask them again.` (no ": <comment>" without one).
  - It can be dismissed while the person is still answering.
  - In an Initiative's Thread, a dismissal recorded while the person works goes along with any other answers waiting for the turn's end; alone, it starts no turn and waits for the user's next message.
  - A fresh session's Brief doesn't list it as open.
  - Allowed only for an open Decision from a person the user talks to: a chat, a person's or the Planner's Thread on a Task, an Initiative's shaping or planning Thread.
  - Refused for an approval, The Office's own Decisions, a Decision in a Stage Thread (a Bug Task Decision among them) (its Stage waits on the answer), one put to a Consultant, and one already answered or closed.
  - Only the user dismisses: an Agent's `answer_decision` can't.
  - It is kept as Ask me again marked dismissed, so the release before reads it as asked again.
- Otherwise the whole form reaches the asking session together, as its next message.
  - On Submit: each question in order with the answer (the option picked, a choice of several's options picked joined by "; ", or the words) and its comment, then the general comment, and who answered.
  - On Ask me again: that the user asked again, with the general comment and who asked.
- A redirect reply ("ask the CTO") turns the Decision into a Consultation ([2.4.3](2.4.3-consultations.md)).
- An answered Decision, or one asked again or dismissed, isn't reopened: the user changes course by writing to the asking person in their Thread.

## The Bug Task Decision

- An Agent in a Stage that finds an issue too big for its Task, or outside its scope, proposes a Bug Task with the Office action `propose_bug_task` (title, Description, why, and optionally its Project and Caused by), instead of fixing it ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md#the-brief)).
- It opens a Bug Task Decision on the Agent's own Task, its problems checked first as a Proposal's are (an unknown Project or Task); the Agent then ends its Stage with needs_decision and the Task is Waiting on you.
- Its form is the proposed Task's fields, editable (Title, Description, Project, Caused by), and four ways to send it: **Create the Bug Task**, **Don't create it**, **Fix now** and **Ask me again**; each takes the general comment.
- Create the Bug Task makes the Task in the same request, from the form as edited: in Backlog, Kind Bug, Normal priority, created by The Office, its Found while working on the Agent's Task and its Caused by as given ([Bug details](2.1.3-tasks.md#bug-details)).
- Fix now creates no Task: the Agent fixes the issue within its own Task, names it in its Handover note, then goes on with its own work.
- A Bug Task Decision opened before Fix now was offered has no Fix now.
- A Create that is refused, such as for a Caused by Task since removed, keeps the Decision open with the refusal shown.
- The Agent reads which Task was created, as a link with its id, that none was, or, for Fix now, that none was and it fixes the issue within this Task now, with the general comment, and goes on with its own Task.
- It can't be dismissed, as no Stage Thread's Decision can, and an Agent's `answer_decision` can't create the Task: Create the Bug Task is the user's, on its card.
- Agents in a Stage have no `create_task`: this is their only way to a new Task.

## Where a Decision shows

- The form is the one Decision everywhere: a Thread on a Task, a Stage (The Office's own Decisions as a form of one choice), a Consultation (which the Consultant answers as the same form, or an answer given for the user), and an Initiative's shaping and planning Threads.
- All show under Needs you, except a chat's, which shows inline in the chat ([2.4.3](2.4.3-consultations.md#calls-from-chats)).
- On the Task page its card is in Needs you and the Thread shows it in short; Now and the Needs you panel show the same card.
- In a Thread on a Task, Decisions are one at a time, a form counting as one.
- An Initiative's shaping Thread may open one on no Task, one at a time, shown in that Thread and under Needs you with the Initiative's title.
- In an Initiative's planning Thread ([2.1](2.1-work.md), Plan the Initiative) the Planner may open one on no Task in the same way.
- In a planning Thread the Planner may also open several on Tasks, one per Task, each naming one of the Initiative's Tasks and shown on that Task's page as well.
- An answer given while the Planner works is recorded at once and handed to its session as its turn ends.

## Waiting for you

- Now carries a second list beside Needs you, **Waiting for you**: things that wait on the user without blocking anyone.
- Each item links to where it is handled.
- An open Proposal in any Thread (a chat, a Task's Thread, an Initiative's Thread), until it is applied or declined; handled in its Thread; left out once its Task is Done or Dropped or its Initiative ended.
- A pull request The Office opened, while open with failing checks or conflicts; handled on its Task and on the pull request; it leaves once neither holds, or once it is merged or closed.
- Skill updates: every installed Skill with an update available, as one item, leaving out a Skill whose update is already drafted as an open Proposal; handled in Settings › Skills.
- An Office update found, until it is scheduled or applied, and hidden while a restart is scheduled; handled in Settings › Updates.
- A Shaping Initiative whose shaper has answered in its shaping Thread and waits on the user's reply, with no Decision open there; handled in that Thread; it leaves once the user replies or a Decision opens there.
- Each item names its Projects for the Project filter; Skill updates, the Office update and a chat's Proposal are Office-wide, shown whatever the filter.
- Items are ordered oldest first; Skill updates have no time and come last.
- It has its own count, never added to Needs you's, and raises no Notification ([2.7](2.7-notifications.md#the-tab-counts-needs-you-and-blinks-for-a-new-item)).
- It refreshes live as the Proposals, pull requests, Skill updates, Office update and Threads behind it change.

## Closing a Decision

- Dropping a Task closes every Decision still open on it, waiting on the user or put to a Consultant.
- A closed Decision leaves Needs you and its Notification clears, and its card says the Task was dropped.
- When an Initiative is done or dropped, every Decision still open on no Task in its shaping or planning Thread closes: it leaves Needs you, its Notification clears, and its card reads "Closed: the Initiative ended".

## Answered for the user

- In any Thread the user talks to a person in (a chat, a person's Thread on a Task, the Planner's, an Initiative's Threads), the user may ask that person to answer a Decision waiting on the user.
- The person does it with the Office tool `answer_decision`, by its id (`read_task` lists a Task's Decisions waiting on the user, each question's options numbered, a choice of several marked as one).
- It either submits the Decision, with each question's answer (an option by its number, a choice of several's options by their numbers, or words), its comment and the general comment, or asks again with the general comment alone, as the user's two ways of sending it.
- The Office trusts the person's word that the user asked, with no confirm click.
- The record is honest: the Decision and its answer line keep who answered and the Thread the user asked in.
- Its card reads "Answered by <person> at your request", linking to that Thread; the line names the person instead of You; the asking person is told so.
- Every rule of the user's own answer holds, and to a Consultation and the Pipeline it is the user's answer.
- It is refused for:
  - a Decision put to a Consultant;
  - an approval of a call past a Hard switch or of an MCP tool (the user's own click, [6](6-permissions-and-safety.md));
  - a Decision in the person's own Thread.
- Agent Stages and Consultations have no such tool.

## Defaults

### Agents wait on the user without blocking a tool call

- "Open a Decision" and consult return at once and the Agent ends its turn.
- The answer arrives as the session's next message, continued through the Context policy ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)): a Decision's whole form at once, submitted or asked again.

### A Decision belongs to the asking Agent's Thread

- It is not a Thread of its own: a Thread is one engine session, and the answer goes into the asking session.
- On the Task page its card is in Needs you.

### The Decision on Stages waiting for a Stand-in Engine

- "Decision: switch Stages to a Stand-in Engine" is The Office's own Decision, and unlike a Decision of an Agent's Thread it is Office-wide, not in one Thread.
- It is one card for every Stage waiting for the user's choice ([2.3](2.3.2-flows.md#a-refused-turn-is-no-failure), "A refused turn is no failure"; [5.4](5.4.6-stand-in-engines-and-usage-windows.md#stand-in-engines), "Stand-in Engines").
- It is answered Stage by Stage, with Switch now on a row, and Switch all now with two or more rows, not once.
- It has no table and closes on its own, when the last Stage has gone on, been resumed, stopped or dropped.
- It shows in Needs you on Now and narrowed on that Task's page ([2.7](2.7-notifications.md#defaults)).
