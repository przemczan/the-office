# 2.4.2 Decisions

Covers: the Decision form, Submit and Ask me again, where a Decision shows, closing a Decision, answering for the user, waiting without blocking.

## The Decision form

- A Decision is a form the Agent opens in the Thread it works in.
- It has one or more **questions** in the order the Agent asks them, its context, and any of the Task's or the Initiative's Attachments it is about.
- A question is a **choice**: its options, with the Agent's recommended one preselected and the reason it gives for it.
- A question is otherwise an **open question**, answered in words.
- Every question takes an optional **comment**, and the form takes one **general comment**.
- A choice is answered by picking an option, or, picking none, by its comment alone, which is then the answer in words.
- The user may ask follow-ups in the same Thread before sending it.
- A Decision recorded before the form reads as a form of one question: a choice with its options, or an open question when it had none.

## Sending a Decision

- **Submit** sends every question's answer with its comment, and the general comment.
  - It needs an answer to every question.
  - The Decision is answered.
- **Ask me again** sends only the general comment, no answers, saying what to explain or change.
  - The Decision closes as **asked again**, leaves Needs you and its Notification clears.
  - The Agent explains and asks again in a new form.
  - The Office's own Decisions (a Stage out of tries, a budget, the Definition of Done, a restart loop, a moved branch) have no Ask me again: nobody is there to ask again.
- Either way the whole form reaches the asking session together, as its next message.
  - On Submit: each question in order with the answer (the option picked, or the words) and its comment, then the general comment, and who answered.
  - On Ask me again: that the user asked again, with the general comment and who asked.
- A redirect reply ("ask the CTO") turns the Decision into a Consultation ([2.4.3](2.4.3-consultations.md)).
- An answered Decision, or one asked again, isn't reopened: the user changes course by writing to the asking person in their Thread.

## Where a Decision shows

- The form is the one Decision everywhere: a Thread on a Task, a Stage (The Office's own Decisions as a form of one choice), a Consultation (which the Consultant answers as the same form, or an answer given for the user), and an Initiative's shaping and planning Threads.
- All show under Needs you, except a chat's, which shows inline in the chat ([2.4.3](2.4.3-consultations.md#calls-from-chats)).
- On the Task page its card is in Needs you and the Thread shows it in short; Now and the Needs you panel show the same card.
- In a Thread on a Task, Decisions are one at a time, a form counting as one.
- An Initiative's shaping Thread may open one on no Task, one at a time, shown in that Thread and under Needs you with the Initiative's title.
- In an Initiative's planning Thread ([2.1](2.1-work.md), Plan the Initiative) the Planner may open one on no Task in the same way.
- In a planning Thread the Planner may also open several on Tasks, one per Task, each naming one of the Initiative's Tasks and shown on that Task's page as well.
- An answer given while the Planner works is recorded at once and handed to its session as its turn ends.

## Closing a Decision

- Dropping a Task closes every Decision still open on it, waiting on the user or put to a Consultant.
- A closed Decision leaves Needs you and its Notification clears, and its card says the Task was dropped.
- When an Initiative is done or dropped, every Decision still open on no Task in its shaping or planning Thread closes: it leaves Needs you, its Notification clears, and its card reads "Closed: the Initiative ended".

## Answered for the user

- In any Thread the user talks to a person in (a chat, a person's Thread on a Task, the Planner's, an Initiative's Threads), the user may ask that person to answer a Decision waiting on the user.
- The person does it with the Office tool `answer_decision`, by its id (`read_task` lists a Task's Decisions waiting on the user, each question's options numbered).
- It either submits the Decision, with each question's answer (an option by its number, or words), its comment and the general comment, or asks again with the general comment alone, as the user's two ways of sending it.
- The Office trusts the person's word that the user asked, with no confirm click.
- The record is honest: the Decision and its answer line keep who answered and the Thread the user asked in.
- Its card reads "Answered by <person> at your request", linking to that Thread; the line names the person instead of You; the asking person is told so.
- Every rule of the user's own answer holds, and to a Consultation and the Pipeline it is the user's answer.
- It is refused for:
  - a Decision put to a Consultant;
  - an approval of a call past a Hard switch or of an MCP tool (the user's own click, [6](6-permissions-and-safety.md));
  - a Decision in the person's own Thread.
- Agent Stages and Consultations have no such tool.

## Defaults

### Agents wait on the user without blocking a tool call

- "Open a Decision" and consult return at once and the Agent ends its turn.
- The answer arrives as the session's next message, continued through the Context policy ([2.5](2.5-context-policy.md)): a Decision's whole form at once, submitted or asked again.

### A Decision belongs to the asking Agent's Thread

- It is not a Thread of its own: a Thread is one engine session, and the answer goes into the asking session.
- On the Task page its card is in Needs you.

### The Decision on Stages waiting for a Stand-in Engine

- "Decision: switch Stages to a Stand-in Engine" is The Office's own Decision, and unlike a Decision of an Agent's Thread it is Office-wide, not in one Thread.
- It is one card for every Stage waiting for the user's choice ([2.3](2.3.2-flows.md#a-refused-turn-is-no-failure), "A refused turn is no failure"; [5.4](5.4.6-stand-in-engines-and-usage-windows.md#stand-in-engines), "Stand-in Engines").
- It is answered Stage by Stage, with Switch now on a row, and Switch all now with two or more rows, not once.
- It has no table and closes on its own, when the last Stage has gone on, been resumed, stopped or dropped.
- It shows in Needs you on Now and narrowed on that Task's page ([2.7](2.7-notifications.md#defaults)).
