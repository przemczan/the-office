# People: module map

**Owns:** Roles hired from presets (Planner, Developer, Reviewer, Office Manager) or from scratch (keyed from the card's title by `roleKeyFor`, never a preset's key), Role cards and their versions (`roles`, `role_card_versions`), authority and Hard switches, Task limits; each person's Memory (`memory_notes`, append-only notes, deleted one at a time or all).

**Talks to:** calls Engine (profiles) and Skills (a Role's skills). Work and Pipelines read cards through their `KnownPeople` ports, bound to `RoleCards` in [app.module.ts](../../app.module.ts); Conversations reads them through the index.

**Traps:** a person let go keeps the Role's key (`letGoAt`), so nothing else can be hired into it until `hireBack`; `RoleCards.find` and `forResumedSession` treat them as not filling it, while `RoleService.read`/`list` still show them. A Role hired from a preset has the preset's fixed key, so only `adapters/http/roles.controller.test.ts` hires from the presets ([testing](../../../docs/testing.md)).
