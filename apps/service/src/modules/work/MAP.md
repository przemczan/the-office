# Work: module map

**Owns:** Ideas, Tasks (states, priority, manual order, Plan, Definition of Done, the Task's Pipeline id) and Attachments (`ideas`, `tasks`, `attachments`); Office settings in one row (`work_settings`, the Office-wide limit; the repository answers the default until Settings saves it); screenshots attached from Stages (`StageScreenshots`); the Office tools for Ideas and Tasks.

**Talks to:** through ports bound in [app.module.ts](../../app.module.ts): `KnownProjects` to `ProjectService`, `ProjectWorktrees` to `TaskWorktrees`, `KnownPeople` to `RoleCards`, `KnownPipelines` to `PipelineLibrary`, `SessionPeople`, `SessionTasks` and `TaskThreads` to `ThreadService`, `TaskDecisions` to `DecisionService`. Pipelines reacts to its events and moves started Tasks through `TaskService`.

**Traps:** the Task holds only its Pipeline's id; the Task's own copy of its Stages lives in Pipelines (`task_stages`), as decided with the user in task 10.
