# Busy-work: module map

## Owns

- The work going on now that a scheduled Office update or restart waits for: `BusyWork` (in memory; `begin` with its `EndBusyWork`, `during`, `list`, `BusyWorkChanged`).
- The hold a scheduled exit puts on new work: `ScheduledExitHold` (one `ScheduledExit`, `waitWords()`, `isScheduled()`). `BusyWorkModule` provides and exports both.

## Talks to

- Nothing but the platform (`EventBus`, `DomainEvent`). Engine, Conversations, Pipelines, Projects, Run environments, Actions and Work import `BusyWorkModule` from `index.ts`; Office updates sets the hold through `ScheduledExits`.

## Traps

- It never imports Office updates (a cycle with Engine).
- `BusyWork.begin` announces in the background, so a counted turn never waits on reactions.
- `ScheduledExitHold.hold` is for Office updates' `ScheduledExits` alone; `ScheduledExit.target` is `unknown` here and cast back by its owner.
