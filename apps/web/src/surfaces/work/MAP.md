A Task's conversation has nothing to open in Shaping, as in Backlog.

A card's working mark never gets narrower than its live dot, avatars and "+N", and keeps the right end of the row of marks (`.board-card-working`, a basis of 0), also when a full row wraps. The Stage's label before the avatars takes only the room left and is `contain: inline-size`, so it never adds to that least width; it grows only on `.working-mark-with-stage`, so a mark without it (the Initiatives tab's) stays at the right. A popover lies on the body, outside the card, yet React passes its events up the card's tree: the card's handlers (`onDragStart`, the row's press and click) act only on what the card's own DOM holds.

An Initiative's Publishing Tasks (`madeFor` `publishing`, `isPublishingTask` in `initiative-words.ts`) are left out of its Tasks block and graph (`ordinaryTasksOf`) while its progress counts them; the Pipelines card shows each in its Project's block (`publishingTasksOf`) with its state and link, and a started one's open blocker as "Waits for “<blocker>” before its next Stage." (`publishingWaitWords`), which the Task page's rail shows too (`PublishingWait`). A Publishing Task's Publish line in the Edit Stages dialog reads "puts initiative/x into main by …" (`taskPublishingWords`, which takes the Task).

A Task's and an Initiative's head actions and an Idea card's options are each a `RecordAction` (`record-actions.tsx`), defined once by a hook beside what it does (`useTaskActions` in `task-actions.ts` gathers the Task's; `InitiativeActions` the Initiative's), and shown twice: as buttons in `RecordActions`, one line that hides (`visibility`, `inert`) whatever wraps past it, the last first, and as the first items of the record's More menu (`withActionItems`), which so always holds every action. An action with a `control` shows that in the row instead of a plain button (a link, Plan the Initiative with its switch, Move up's split button), `null` for one only the menu lists (Move to top). A board card's `TaskMenu` gets no head actions and lists its planning ones (`usePlanningActions`).

An Initiative's Drop asks first (`drop-initiative-dialog.tsx`): it names how many of its Tasks, from the board's list, are not yet Done or Dropped and how many of them are started (`droppedWithIt`, `dropTasksWords`), and keeps those counts from the click on, as the Tasks' own events land before the answer and would change the toast (`droppedWords`).
The Task graph's edge routing (`routeEdges`) is fitted to the 44px column gap of `.task-graph-columns` (`EDGE_ROUTING`: 6 out, 6 corner, 12 of bends, 6 corner, 4 in, 8 head, 2 tip gap): a change to that gap or to the graph's column order (`TaskBox.column` comes from `taskGraph`'s columns) changes where the bends land. Each arrowhead is a path of its own, never an SVG marker, so it keeps its line's colour.

An Initiative's block is a `group`, never a region or a heading: the e2e tests find a column by its region and its `h3`, and count `.board`'s regions, so a block keeps them true; a lookup of another `group` by name (the filter bar's Layout) must be exact. In a column with blocks the drop reads the block under the pointer from the DOM (`data-initiative-block`), and a card lands before the card under the pointer or the next one of its block (or of the loose cards) below it (`landingBefore`), as a block's head and the gaps between blocks hold no card.
