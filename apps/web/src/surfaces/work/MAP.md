# Work surface: map

**Owns:** the Work board (the Project filter narrows its cards, the Tags and Initiatives its bar offers, Ideas with their tab's count, and Initiatives, each with the shell's note of how many more are in other Projects; the board's counts only the Tasks its filters match, and blockers and drag placement still read every Task; each card's Tags; its filters, kept in the address, in `board-filters.ts` with the bar in `filter-bar.tsx`, the search's words matched by the service's `GET /tasks?query=`; its Saved views in `saved-views.tsx`, a view on while the filters are its own, `sameBoardFilters`), a Task's Tags (`tags-input.tsx`, on the Task page in `task-tags.tsx` and in "+ Task"; suggested from the Tasks list, `tag-words.ts`), Ideas (shaped into a Task or an Initiative), Initiatives (the tab's cards with progress, `initiatives-list.tsx`; the Initiative page, `initiative-page.tsx`: the Task graph across Projects, `task-graph.tsx`, laid out in `initiative-words.ts`, blockers outside it dashed; its head's Confirm, "Add a Task" (the new-Task dialog given the Initiative, with what it must wait for) and Drop, `initiative-actions.tsx`; the outcome the user edits while shaping, `outcome-block.tsx`; its record and its Threads with the CTO), and the Task page: the record (Overview, Changes), planning, run controls, Stage rows, its dependencies (added and removed before it starts; the board's "Blocked by"), the "Edit for this Task" dialog (the shell's Stage editor on the Task's copy, and the Pipeline swap before Start), the Gate block and the Task's conversation. The board's card is one link (the title's, stretched over it; its menu, buttons and links sit above), its "More" menu the shell's `FloatingMenu`. The Changes tab folds each file to its header, its part of the diff found by `diffOfFile`, with "Unfold all" and "Fold all". "+ Task" opens the new Task's page; "Add a Task" on an Initiative stays there. The queue for Start (`queue-parts.tsx`, from `GET /queue`): a queued Task's "Queued" mark with its place and reasons on its board card and on its page, where "Move up" and "Take out of the queue" stand in for Start; Start says when it would only join the queue (`start-action.tsx`). The Usage estimate (the shell's `UsageEstimateLine`): on each Ready card, queued ones too, from `GET /usage-estimates`; beside Start and in the Ready block, from the start check; the Planner's token estimate under the Plan. Overlaps: the Plan's files to change under the Plan, edited and confirmed with it (`PlanBlock`'s `PlanEditor` in `planning-parts.tsx`); an Overlap in the start check names the Task in progress and the files, and Start becomes "Start anyway" and "Queue behind it" (`start-action.tsx`). A Task's and an Initiative's page share the Threads beside the record (`record-threads.tsx`; a Thread added while the page is open, such as a Stage's session, gets its tab after the shown one, marked New, without being shown, `thread-tabs.ts`), Attachments (`attachments-block.tsx`, in the shell's gallery: a Task's by round from its Stages, an Initiative's and an Idea's card's in one; the Gate block shows only the current round, the earlier ones staying on the record), the origin Idea and the missing-record view. It reads the service through `src/api/` (see [the web app's map](../../MAP.md)).
