## Owns

- Now, the home: Needs you (the shell's `NeedsYouFeed`, the Stand-in choice card), Waiting for you (`WaitingForYouGroup`), Running, Agents (`working-agents.ts`, from the Live view's snapshot), the Queue, Shaping, Since you left and today's tokens; the fire alarm panel (`alarm-panel.tsx`) above them while it is on.

## Talks to

- The one `now` query (replaced whole by `now.changed`), `liveViewQueries.snapshot` (`live-view.changed`), `nowQueries.sinceYouLeft` (each user's own, read again on `now.changed`), Work's `GET /usage-estimates`; through `src/api/` (see [the web app's map](../../MAP.md)).

## Traps

- Waiting for you never counts in Needs you's heading.
- The Project filter narrows the feeds (an Initiative with no Tasks yet, and a chat, always show); desks and tokens stay Office-wide.
- An Off-board Task's out-of-tries Decision shows as the shell's "Off-board Task failed" card; its Retry answers the first option with no guidance.
