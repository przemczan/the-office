# Stats surface: map

**Owns:** the Stats page (between Projects and the Live view on the rail, icon `chart-column`; spec 2.11, [the design](../../../../../docs/design/stats.html) is its UI spec), in two tabs under one head (`stats-tabs.tsx`'s `StatsTabs`: packages/ui's `Tabs` navigating as Work's do, the tab's range picker at the right end of their row, wrapping under them as a `Select` on a narrow page, and the top bar's crumb "Stats › <tab>"): **Tokens and cost** at `/stats` (`stats-page.tsx`), the basic stats always and the full stats from Grafana once it answers, and **System health** at `/stats/system-health` (below). Each block is `stats-parts.tsx`'s `StatsBlock`, a `Card` in a `section` named by its `h3`, its `BlockLoader` (`rows`, `bars` or `lines`) until its first answer; a failed read is an `Alert`. `stats-page.tsx` lays them on a 12-column grid by the full stats' state (`GET /stats/full`'s `state`):

- **Full:** the range picker (24 h, 7 d, 30 d) on the tabs' row, kept in the address as `?range=` (`useStatsRange`, the router's `StatsSearch`, 7 d left out), the coverage line under it, then the full blocks in the design's order, then Usage windows now and This week side by side; Tokens per day and the top Tasks give way to the full versions. Until the first answer, the full layout and its loaders show where a Grafana is chosen (no message from `statsMessageOf`); a new range or split keeps the last answer (`keepPreviousData`) with the full blocks' loaders.
  - **Tokens and estimated cost over time** (`usage-over-time.tsx`): Tokens / Estimated cost and the split (model, Role, Stage, Pipeline, Project, sent as `split=`), the five largest values and Other stacked per step by `BarsChart`, a legend with each value's total, the head "4.2M tokens · $38.10 estimated · 7 days", each step's `Tooltip`, Show as a table. A group named "" reads `EMPTY_GROUP_WORDS` ("Not in a Stage", "No Pipeline", "No Project", "No Role", "No model").
  - **The most expensive** (`costliest-work.tsx`): Tasks / Initiatives, each row a link to `/tasks/<id>` or `/work/initiatives/<id>`, its `ProjectChip`s, estimated cost, tokens and a bar against the first; its Stage runs, retries and "Retries: $4.10, 18%" counted from its tries; the fold (`aria-expanded`) gives each Stage's tries ("Run 2 · retry · $0.90") and "Outside a Stage", or an Initiative's Tasks and "The Initiative's Threads".
  - **Usage windows over time** (`windows-over-time.tsx`), "Office-wide": one engine at a time, the fullest now first, its 5-hour and 7-day lines with 90% and 100% guides on the range's time axis, one tab stop stepping each reading's `Tooltip`, Show as a table; under it "What was running on <engine>": one row per Task of its Stage runs, each bar its Project's mark colour (`projectColourClass`), a link to the Task with a `Tooltip` (when, Task, Stage), and its own table.
- **Not on:** the basic stats as before, with **the message** at the foot by Settings › Telemetry (Off, an own endpoint, set by the environment), each linking to `/settings/telemetry`.
- **Starting, couldn't start, unreachable, refused, no data:** `FullStatsAlert` above the basic stats (as before, no message); couldn't start, unreachable and refused link to Settings › Telemetry, unreachable has Try again (reads again). The full stats are read every minute, every 3 s while Grafana starts.

**System health** (`system-health.tsx`, Office-wide throughout, read by `statsQueries.systemHealth(range)`): the range (1 h, 24 h, 7 d, 30 d) in the address as `?range=` (1 h left out), the source line (1 h: since when and "Updated … · every 5 seconds" with a live dot; "Last answer …" after a failed read), **Load now** on every range, then `health-blocks.tsx`'s **Responsiveness** (p99 and Max, the 100 ms guide, amber from 100 ms), **CPU and memory** (three charts, stacked on a narrow page), **Requests** and **Database**, each with Show as a table, and `slowest-table.tsx`'s slowest routes and queries (by Statement shape on 1 h, by operation and table on the longer ranges): sorted by total time, each figure column's head a sort button with `aria-sort`, largest first, the first 10 then Show all, a plain `table` since `DataTable` can't carry `aria-sort` or sort one way; on 1 h a query's row is clamped to 2 lines and unfolds to its whole shape with `statement-fold.tsx`'s Copy and Called from. Every chart is `line-chart.tsx`'s `LineChart`: a gap where points are more than 1.5 steps apart or a figure is null, the time before the first point hatched ("Before this start" on 1 h, "Nothing sent yet" otherwise), dashed guides. A read that fails shows "Couldn't read System health" with Try again above the last answer; on the longer ranges the Grafana states reuse `FullStatsAlert`, `statsMessageOf` and `SettingsLink`, with a card whose "Show the last hour" picks 1 h, and an `unansweredBlocks` block shows Unreachable's words. Its words and figure formats are in `system-health-words.ts`.

The basic blocks: **Tokens per day**, "Office-wide", the 30 office days stacked by kind (input, output, cache writes) through `BarsChart`, the cache reads in its strip under it, Show as a table, the head two `TokenCount`s (cache reads left out, `countedTokens`); **The Tasks with the most tokens this week**, each a link with its `ProjectChip`, tokens and a bar, and the hint that only Stage runs count; **Usage windows now**, "Office-wide", each engine whose windows are `read` or `not-read`, each a meter toned by the strip's `usageTone`; **This week**, Tasks done and Stage retries since `weekStartsAt`.

`BarsChart` (`stats-parts.tsx`) is the one stacked bar chart and `LineChart` the one line chart (Usage windows over time and System health): one tab stop, ← → (Shift for ten), Home and End step a point's `Tooltip`, through the shared `useChartPick`; hovering a `LineChart` picks the nearest point, its one held-open `Tooltip` re-placed as the point moves. `Picker` is a `SegmentedControl` in a named `role="group"`, a `Select` in its place on a narrow page (`@container page`, stats.css). The "Office-wide" badge carries a `Tooltip`, "The Project filter doesn't narrow this…", only while a Project filter is set. Estimated cost is always called estimated, in US dollars (`usdWords`). Every word is in `stats-words.ts` or `system-health-words.ts`; `stats.css` draws with tokens only.

**Talks to:** `api/telemetry.ts`'s `statsQueries.basic(filter)` (`GET /stats/basic`) and `statsQueries.full(filter, range, split)` (`GET /stats/full?range=&split=`, one `project` per Project the filter shows), `statsQueries.systemHealth(range)` (`GET /stats/system-health?range=`), and `telemetryQueries.settings()`; `projectQueries.list()` for the chips and the lane's colours; the shell's `useProjectFilter`, `TokenCount`, `tokensShort`, `ProjectChip`, `BlockLoader`, the strip's window words (`usageTone`, `meterFilled`, `percentWords`) and `time.ts`.

**Traps:** an office day (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and a full-stats step of a day or more (the service's steps start at midnight UTC) are worded in UTC, so they never shift with the browser's time zone; shorter steps, the resets, "since" and the lane are instants, worded in the browser's. The service's step isn't the range's: 7 d goes by 6 hours, so `usage-over-time.tsx` takes it from the answer's first two steps. The contract carries no date of the first data point, so the coverage line names none and nothing is hatched "Nothing sent yet". A Tooltip reads its size through `ResizeObserver`, which the tests stub for jsdom; the segmented controls' inline styles break jsdom's `cloneNode`, so the full stats' test finds buttons without `shownText`.
