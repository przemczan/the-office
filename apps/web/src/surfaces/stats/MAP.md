# Stats surface: map

## Owns

- The Stats page ([the design](../../../../../docs/design/stats.html) is its UI spec): two tabs (`stats-tabs.tsx`).
- **Tokens and cost** at `/stats` (`stats-page.tsx`: basic stats always, full stats from Grafana once it answers, laid out by `GET /stats/full`'s `state`)
- **System health** at `/stats/system-health` (`system-health.tsx`, `health-blocks.tsx`, `slowest-table.tsx`, `statement-fold.tsx`).
- Full blocks: `usage-over-time.tsx`, `costliest-work.tsx`, `windows-over-time.tsx`; basic blocks: Tokens per day, top Tasks, Usage windows now, This week.
- Shared parts: `stats-parts.tsx` (`StatsBlock`, `BarsChart`, `Picker`), `line-chart.tsx` (`LineChart`), `useChartPick`, `FullStatsAlert`, `statsMessageOf`; words in `stats-words.ts` and `system-health-words.ts`; `stats.css` (tokens only).

## Talks to

- `api/telemetry.ts` (`statsQueries.basic`, `.full`, `.systemHealth`, `telemetryQueries.settings()`), `projectQueries.list()`; the shell's `useProjectFilter`, `TokenCount`, `tokensShort`, `ProjectChip`, `BlockLoader`, strip window words, `time.ts`. See [the web app's map](../../MAP.md).

## Traps

- Office days and full-stats steps of a day or more are worded in UTC; shorter steps, resets, "since" and the lane are instants in the browser's zone.
- The service's step isn't the range's (7 d goes by 6 h): take it from the answer's first two steps.
- Until telemetry settings are read, show loaders and choose no layout (never the basic charts); a new range or split keeps the last answer (`keepPreviousData`).
- The contract has no first data point date: nothing is hatched "Nothing sent yet" on the full stats.
- The slowest table is a plain `table` (`DataTable` can't carry `aria-sort`).
- Estimated cost is always called estimated, in US dollars (`usdWords`).
- Tests stub `ResizeObserver` for Tooltips; segmented controls' inline styles break jsdom's `cloneNode`, so find buttons without `shownText`.
