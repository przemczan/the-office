# Live view surface: map

## Owns

- The Live view page (`/live-view`; [the adopted design](../../../../../docs/design/live-view.html) is its UI spec): The Office as an isometric pixel-art office.
- Pure parts: art (`art/`: `pixel-grid.ts`, `palettes.ts`, `floor-art.ts`, `figures.ts`, `overlays.ts`; `CREDITS.md`), the floor plan (`floor-plan.ts`), the hours (`light.ts`, generated `zones.json`), pastimes (`pastimes.ts`, `seeded.ts`), states and words (`live-states.ts`), walks (`routes.ts`, `walkers.ts`), glances and sips (`glances.ts`), the view (`view.ts`, `figure-hit.ts`).
- The stage (`office-stage.ts`, `stage-scene.ts`, `stage-textures.ts`, `stage-input.ts`, PixiJS) and the rest of the page (`time-chip.tsx`, `nameplate.tsx`, `people-list.tsx`, `work-links.tsx`, `press-words.ts`, `use-reduced-motion.ts`, `live-view.css`).

## Talks to

- `api/live-view.ts` (`liveViewQueries.snapshot()`, kept whole by `live-view.changed`; only this page reads it), People's `roleQueries.list()`, the shell's `nowQueries.now()` for the fire alarm. See [the web app's map](../../MAP.md).

## Traps

- pixi.js loads only through the dynamic `import("./office-stage.ts")`: nothing imported statically may import pixi.js or the stage files; page tests mock `office-stage.ts`. Without WebGL the page says so and opens the list.
- Art colours are `0xRRGGBB` numbers, an exception to spec 4.1's tokens only; clothes' tint is read from the ramp tokens.
- Zoom is a whole number of device pixels per art pixel with nearest-neighbour textures; never set a scale `view.ts` didn't give; the in-between fit goes through the RenderTexture. Canvas positions use `view.level`. `officeBounds` stays the room.
- Depth: furniture blocks every tile it stands on; long furniture sorts by its front-left corner; glows are additive sprites sorted with their item (`DESK_DEPTHS`), never a layer; a figure keeps the seat's depth until a step away.
- The ticker runs only while open, visible and without reduced motion; the still office renders on change only. Reduced motion remakes the stage (`canvasKey`).
- `lookOf`'s draw order must never change (the same Role key draws the same figure everywhere).
- The floor is laid out once per page open; nobody moves on a hire. `apply` is called only once the snapshot and the Roles have both loaded (else everyone walks in as hires).
- Routes are searched only in `walkers.direct` and `setFloor`, never per frame; a frame allocates nothing per walker. A mid-walk course change starts from a walkable tile.
- Nameplates of walking figures move by writing `style.transform` into the DOM, never React state; outside the room a figure is drawn in the doorway container.
- Conversations and desk activity come from the walkers, not the snapshot's placement.
- The place comes only from `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`: no geolocation, no setting, nothing sent (`light.test.ts` checks). `zones.json` is generated (`npm run live-view-zones`), never hand-edited.
- Picks are seeded by the Role key and the turn, never `Math.random()`; dawn resets pastime turns; walking at light changes happens only when the crossing is seen live.
- Untinted on purpose: lit screens, lamp bulb, sky, exit sign, "+N" tag, overlays.
- A figure's size comes from its pose (`figureBoundsOf`); `sit-back-play` is drawn in profile.
- A let-go is told apart by the snapshot's keys, never by a `gone` placement.
